# Source map

Where to look, in rough order of evidential weight.

## Forecast aggregators and markets

| Source | URL | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Metaculus | metaculus.com | Primary. Use the `metaculus-aggregations` skill; take CP + Pro + gold. |
| Kalshi | kalshi.com | Real-money, CFTC-regulated, US-centric. Good on macro/econ/politics. |
| Polymarket | polymarket.com | Real-money, deep on geopolitics/elections. Check volume; long-dated contracts carry a time-value discount. |
| Manifold | manifold.markets | Play-money. **Less accurate**, especially thin markets. Use as idea source, discount as evidence. |
| Good Judgment Open | gjopen.com | Crowd forecasts with a track record. |
| INFER | infer-pub.com | US-policy oriented crowd forecasting. |
| Sentinel Team | open.substack.com/pub/sentinelteam | Weekly global-catastrophic-risk forecasts with explicit numbers. |
| Mantic | blog.mantic.com | Forecasting-model write-ups and comparisons. |

## Primary data

- Statistical offices: Destatis, Eurostat, BLS, BEA, ONS, national central banks.
- IMF WEO, World Bank, OECD, UN.
- Registries and trackers: ClinicalTrials.gov, EMA/FDA approvals, Our World in Data,
  ACLED (conflict), IAEA, EIA, Copernicus/NOAA (climate), arXiv/Semantic Scholar,
  Epoch AI (AI compute/model trends), Stanford AI Index.
- Company filings: SEC EDGAR, Bundesanzeiger.

## Literature

Peer-reviewed work beats commentary. PubMed, bioRxiv/medRxiv, arXiv, NBER, SSRN.
Note preprint status when citing a preprint.

## News

Prefer wire services and specialist trade press over general commentary:
Reuters, AP, AFP, Bloomberg, FT, Politico/Politico Europe, Nikkei, specialist outlets
per domain. For paywalled pages, retry the URL prefixed with `https://archive.is/`.

## Recording sources

Numbered list at the bottom of `forecast.md`: title, URL, access date.
Snapshot anything volatile (prices, aggregations, series) into `questions/<slug>/raw/`
with a dated filename — a URL that changes underneath you is not a citation.
