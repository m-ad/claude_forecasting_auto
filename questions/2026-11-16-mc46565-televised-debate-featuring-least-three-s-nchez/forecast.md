## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

YES if a programme on a nationwide Spanish TV channel, starting between 00:00 and 23:59 CET on Thursday 26 November 2026, has at least 3 of these 4 people debating in person in the same studio session: Sánchez, Feijóo, Abascal, Colau. Substitutes do not count toward the three, and they don't disqualify the programme either. So a four-way with one leader absent or replaced still resolves YES (4 − 1 = 3). A spokespeople panel (e.g. laSexta "Mesa Abierta") or a two-way does not count. A late-evening start (22:00 CET is typical) on the 26th counts, even if the programme runs past midnight. The source is the Doble Lectura tracker, falling back to broadcaster listings or credible media. The 26th is the second-to-last campaign day; the campaign ends at 24:00 on 27 November.

### Outside view

Reference class: Spanish general elections since 2015 (2015, 2016, April 2019, November 2019, 2023; n = 5). Every one had at least one televised multi-candidate debate with at least three of the main national leaders. In 2015, Atresmedia's four-way had Sánchez, Rivera and Iglesias, with Santamaría standing in for Rajoy. In 2023 RTVE ran a three-way with Sánchez, Díaz and Abascal while Feijóo boycotted. The specific date is the weak link: dates are often fixed only days ahead and they slip. The cara a cara here already moved from 16 to 19 November. Prior that such a debate happens at all: about 90%. Prior that it lands on a date that both main parties had already named: about 70–75%.

### Inside view

- On 14 November EFE, citing sources from the negotiating teams, reported that the Academia de Televisión's multi-candidate debate is set for Thursday 26 November. The expected line-up is Sánchez, Feijóo, Abascal and Colau, with the same five thematic blocks as the 19 November cara a cara [3][4]. On 15 November Vozpópuli reported that the Academia organises it and RTVE broadcasts it [2].
- Both main parties want this date. The PP asked for a four-way on the 26th (at first on Atresmedia) [6]. The PSOE has wanted a four-way on the 26th since 22 October (at first on TVE) [7]. The dispute was over the broadcaster, and it was settled when both accepted the Academia [8]. Atresmedia moved its own four-way to the 23rd so as not to clash with the 26th [9]. Colau reportedly dropped a Cornellà rally on the 26th because of the debate [10].
- The three-of-four threshold absorbs the most common failure, one leader pulling out. Feijóo and Abascal have both committed to four-ways, and Colau's party says it will attend every debate.
- Remaining risks. (a) The Academia has not officially confirmed the date or time; Doble Lectura still lists the debate as unconfirmed [1]. (b) The Junta Electoral Central (JEC) ruling on Podemos could reopen the format. The PSOE says Alvise should be added if Podemos is, but a wider debate still resolves YES. (c) The date could slip to the 25th or 27th, or the debate could collapse late. I put (a)+(c) at about 12% and other failures (a dispute over a substitute, nothing airing) at about 3%.
- Net: 85%.

### What would change my mind

- The Academia announces the second debate for a date other than the 26th, or a party withdraws so that two of the four are missing. Either would push this below 20%.
- An official Academia/RTVE confirmation of the 26th with a start time would push it to about 93%.
