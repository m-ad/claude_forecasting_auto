## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-19

### What resolves YES

Numeric question. It resolves to the headline consolidated "Net sales" in US$ billions that Amazon.com, Inc. reports for the quarter ended 30 September 2026, as first printed in the earnings release (expected after the close on 29 October 2026; the IR page or 8-K). It does not use segment figures, trailing-twelve-month figures or FX-neutral figures. If the release is not out by the resolution date, the question is annulled. The question's range is 194–212 with both bounds open.

### Inside view

- **Guidance** is $197.0–202.0B, +9% to +12% y/y. It assumes about 80 bp of FX headwind [1][2]. The step-down from Q2's +20% growth is largely mechanical: **Prime Day moved into Q2 2026**. The CFO said Q3 growth would be about 400 bp higher ex-Prime Day timing [1]. That makes the low-looking guide a timing effect, not a slowdown signal.
- **Momentum is strong.** AWS grew 37% in Q2, its fastest in 18 quarters. Advertising grew 26% [1][3]. Q1 2026 ($181.5B, +17%) and Q2 2026 ($200.6B, +20%) both cleared consensus comfortably [3][4]. This argues for the upper half of the historical beat distribution, so I lean slightly above the reference-class centre.
- **FX** was roughly neutral over Q3. DXY was about +0.2% and EUR/USD about −0.3% from 1 Jul to 30 Sep (secondary source) [5]. FX is close to what the guide assumed, with no sizable surprise in either direction.
- **Consensus** is about $202.0–202.1B (S&P Global, Benzinga) [6][7]. Analyst range: $194.2–211.4B.

Median **$203.0B**: the guidance top plus about 0.5%, slightly above consensus. Tails are kept wide (5–95%: 199.6–206.6) because of AWS upside and the uncertain size of the Prime Day shift.

### Evidence

- 2026-07-30 release: Q2 2026 net sales were $200.6B (+20%), beating about $196.4B consensus. AWS was $42.2B (+37%). Q3 guide: $197–202B, op. income $22.5–26.5B. Prime Day moved into Q2, worth about 400 bp of Q3 growth [1][2].
- Q1 2026: $181.5B (+17%, +15% ex-FX). The Q2 guide of $194–199B was beaten by $1.6B [3][4].
- Consensus for Q3 2026 was about $202B as of Aug–Oct 2026, cut 0.9% after the guide [6][7].
- Q3 FX was roughly flat. The dollar's rally came mostly in late Sep–early Oct [5].

### What would change my mind

- How large the Prime Day shift really is. If Q3 2025 Prime Day sales were bigger than the ~400 bp implies, Amazon could land near the guide midpoint (~$200B), as in Q2/Q4 2024.
- A sharp AWS acceleration beyond 37% (capacity coming online) could push the figure to $205B or more.
