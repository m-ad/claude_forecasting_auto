## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-20

### What resolves YES

YES only if, **before 30 October 2026**, Kaiser Permanente and CNA/NNU announce a **full tentative agreement**: a complete successor contract signed off by both bargaining teams and sent to members for a ratification vote. It must cover the ~25,000 Northern California CNA registered nurses whose talks began in July 2026. The announcement can come from Kaiser's NorCal CNA bargaining page, an NNU press release or credible media. These do **not** count: a contract extension, partial agreements on individual articles, a mediator's proposal only one side accepts, continued bargaining, or a TA for another unit (for example the separate Los Angeles Medical Center CNA unit). In practice that leaves about 10 days, 20–29 October.

### Outside view

The reference class is Kaiser–CNA Northern California successor contracts (n = 3 recent cycles) [6][9][10]. None of the three reached a TA within 60 days of expiry.
- **2014–15:** talks began in July 2014, with a 2-day strike in November 2014. The TA came on 16 January 2015, three days before a second noticed strike.
- **2017–18:** the contract expired around 31 October 2017. The TA came on 16 March 2018, about 135 days after expiry, with no strike.
- **2022:** bargaining began in June and the contract expired around 31 August. The strike notice went out on 10 November and the TA came on 17 November, about 78 days after expiry and 4 days before the strike.
- **UNAC/UHCP (2025–26), the parallel Kaiser case:** strikes in October 2025 and January–February 2026, with TAs only in February–March 2026 [8].

In every cycle the TA came on a strike deadline or after months of drift. As of 20 October the contract had been expired for 50 days, with no notice served. Base rate for a TA in the next 10 days: about 3–6%.

### Inside view

The specific facts push below that base rate:
- **Economics barely started.** On 7 October Kaiser said neither side had made an opening wage proposal when CNA scheduled its 9 strike-vote dates [3]. A full contract would need wages, staffing and AI language all closed within about four sessions.
- **Few sessions are scheduled.** After the 7 October session (the 17th), the next sessions are 21 and 22 October. Kaiser is "working to confirm additional dates through December" [2], so neither side expects a fast close.
- **The union is escalating.** CNA called a strike authorization vote and agreed to only 3 extra dates [2][3]. As of the latest updates Kaiser had received no 10-day strike notice. A notice served on about 20 October would put a strike deadline near 30 October, so any deadline-driven TA would most likely land on or after the question closes.
- **CNA's pattern** is to use authorization and strike notices as leverage over several weeks, with Sutter (also in bargaining) moving in parallel [4].
- **None of my searches found a TA**, and no outlet reported an imminent deal.

I place this at about 3%. I don't go lower because Kaiser wants "no economic takeaways" and a quick deal after its $1B strike cost in early 2026, so a surprise settlement at the 21/22 October sessions can't be ruled out.

### Evidence

- 12 Oct 2026: Kaiser's NorCal update says the 17th session was on 7 October and the next are on 21–22 October, with more being confirmed through December. Kaiser is "disappointed that CNA has called for a strike authorization vote" [2].
- 7–8 Oct 2026: Kaiser's open letter says it offered "numerous" extra dates and CNA agreed to only 3. It also says CNA set 9 strike-vote dates before either side presented an opening wage proposal [3].
- The NorCal resolution page shows no TA as of 20 October 2026 [1].
- 8 Oct 2026: NNU says more than 35,000 Kaiser and Sutter nurses are in active bargaining, with a joint protest held on 16 September [4].
- The prior contract ran 1 September 2022 to 31 August 2026 [5]. In 2022 the 10-day notice went out on 10 November and the TA followed on 17 November [6].
- 5 Oct 2026: an arbitrator ruled for CNA on wages Kaiser withheld from more than 1,000 nurses. It is a side dispute, adding friction [9].
- 1 Sep 2026: informational pickets at about 22 facilities. The union said wages had not yet been discussed [10].
- The LAMC unit is on a separate track (12 sessions by 9 October, next on 23 October) and does not count toward resolution [7].

### What would change my mind

- A Kaiser or NNU update after the 21–22 October sessions reporting a breakthrough on wages, or the parties adding marathon sessions with a mediator.
- A 10-day strike notice served around 18–20 October. That would raise the chance of a last-minute TA around 28–30 October, though mostly at or after the cutoff.
