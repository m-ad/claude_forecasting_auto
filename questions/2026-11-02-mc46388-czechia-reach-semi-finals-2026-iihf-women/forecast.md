### Outside view

Czechia reached the semi-finals at each of the last four World Championships (2022–2025): 4 of 4. That record came under the old format, though, where the quarter-final opponents were weaker. In 2025, for example, Czechia beat Switzerland 7-0 in the quarter-final [6]. Under the new snake seeding by ranking, a Group A runner-up meets the strongest non-Canadian European team, and third place meets Canada. A better reference class is Czechia's results against Finland and Sweden in single games: roughly even over the last 20 games against Finland (10-10) [7]. Against Sweden, Czechia has lost three times in a row without scoring, including the 2026 Olympic quarter-final, which Sweden won 2-0 [4][8]. Against Canada, Czechia's record in elimination games is close to zero wins.

That puts Czechia at about 57% for second place. Against Finland or Sweden, Czechia is a slight underdog.
