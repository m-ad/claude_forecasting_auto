## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-18

### What resolves YES

The value is the count (0–15) of the 15 listed officials who leave their position, formally announce they will leave, or whose agency announces they have left or will leave, before 1 January 2027. The list: Rubio, Bessent, Hegseth, Bondi, RFK Jr., Lutnick, Noem, Gabbard, Ratcliffe, Vought, Wiles, Patel, Homan, Navarro, Miller. Someone holding two listed roles must leave both. The criteria contain no "after opening" condition. The text "none had turned over at writing" is stale, so I read the criteria literally: departures before the question opened (Noem, Bondi) count.

### Outside view

Trump's first term had 92% turnover among 65 senior positions over four years, with year-3 turnover around 17% (Brookings, cited in the question). Cabinet turnover tends to cluster right after midterms (Sessions 2018, Rumsfeld 2006). Applied to the 12 officials still in post for the last ~2.5 months of 2026, this gives roughly 0.1 departures per person, or about 1.2 in expectation.

### Inside view

Already out (reported): Noem (fired March 2026), Bondi (fired 2 April 2026), Gabbard (resignation announced 22 May 2026). That is 3 certain, assuming the literal reading. I give about 7% to a resolver counting only departures after the 27 April opening (which would give 1 plus later exits); that pulls the lower tail slightly down.

Twelve officials remain. Prediction markets (Kalshi/Polymarket average, 18 Oct, via aggregator) for "leaves in 2026": Wiles 34%, Hegseth 24%, Homan 20%, Patel 17%, Lutnick 16%, RFK Jr. 14%, Vought 14%, Navarro 14%, Miller 13%, Ratcliffe 12%, Bessent 6%, Rubio 6%. They sum to about 1.9, but "leave" markets usually carry a longshot bias and some quotes are stale. I shade the expectation down to about 1.6 additional exits. Departures are correlated through a possible post-midterm (3 Nov) shakeup, so I keep a fat right tail. Distribution of additional exits: 0: 25%, 1: 30%, 2: 22%, 3: 12%, 4: 6%, 5+: 5%. Add 3 for the total.
