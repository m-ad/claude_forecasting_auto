## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-17

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the aggregate number of drones of every type (Shahed/Geran, including the jet-powered ones, plus Gerbera/Parodiya decoys; missiles excluded) that the Ukrainian Air Force reports as launched in its overnight summary for the night of 26–27 November 2026. That summary is published on the morning of 27 Nov (Kyiv time) and normally covers 18:00 on 26 Nov to about 08:00–09:00 on 27 Nov. The figure is taken as reported by Ukrainska Pravda or Ukrinform. If several figures are published, the latest one before 12:00 UTC on 27 Nov counts. A report of no launches resolves 0. The range is 0–600, closed at 0 and open above 600. The target night is 10 nights after the latest observed night (15–16 Nov: 158 drones). It is one draw from a nightly series that is very skewed: the November 2026 nights so far ran 96–317.

### Outside view

The reference class is Ukrainian Air Force overnight totals in autumn 2026. The November 2026 nights with known figures were: 193 (1–2), 289 (2–3), 310 (3–4), 172 (6–7), 164 (7–8), 317 (8–9), 162 (9–10), 163 (10–11), 117 (12–13), 96 (13–14), 132 (14–15) and 158 (15–16). That is n = 12, median about 163, interquartile range about 140–240 and maximum 317. October 2026 daily Air Force data come to about 6,158 drones, roughly 199 a day including daytime launches. The Institute for Science and International Security (ISIS) puts 2026 monthly averages at about 190–263 a day. In November 2025, quiet nights ran about 100–140 and mass-strike nights reached 433, 476 and 596. Base distribution: a median of about 160 with a long right tail.

### Inside view

There are two regimes. On "routine" nights (about 80 %), totals of roughly 90–220 are typical. On "mass-strike" nights (about 15–20 %), Russia combines drones with missiles against the energy grid, at 280–600+. In November 2026 these came every 5–7 days (2–4 Nov, 8–9 Nov). On 16 Nov, Zelensky warned that a new massive strike was imminent for 16–17 Nov. If that strike comes, the next one would plausibly fall around 22–28 Nov, which keeps the target night's chance of being a mass-strike night at a meaningful 15–25 %. The mass-strike nights this month (≤ 317) were smaller than the 2025 peaks, so I put p90 at about 310 and p95 at about 400, leaving a few percent of mass above 450. A ceasefire or energy truce by 26 Nov is unlikely (Abu Dhabi talks produced none, and the Polymarket ceasefire-by-30-Nov price was about 7.5 % in mid-October). It would mainly thin the lower tail, which p5 = 65 partly covers. I made no model; the percentiles are judgement fitted to the mixture.

### What would change my mind

- Whether the 26–27 Nov night lands in a mass-strike cycle. A large strike on about 21–24 Nov would make one on 26–27 less likely; a lull from 18 to 25 Nov would make it more likely.
- An energy truce agreed in the next round of talks, which would shift mass downward sharply.
