## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-19

### What resolves YES

This is a numeric question. It resolves to the single-day (not cumulative) advance-vote figure the NZ Electoral Commission lists against Thursday 29 October 2026 in its 2026 General Election voting-statistics table on vote.nz / elections.nz, read at resolution (scheduled 30 October 2026). Day 4 is the fourth of 12 advance-voting days (26 Oct – 6 Nov). If the table shows ordinary and special votes separately, the two are added; if it shows ordinary votes only, that figure counts. Overseas votes are excluded, and later retrospective additions do not count. Both bounds are open: the 40,000–260,000 range does not cap the outcome. If no daily figure is published, the question is annulled.

### Outside view

The 2023 timeline is the closest analogue: 12 days, starting on a Monday, with election day on the second Saturday. Day 4 (the first Thursday) in the Commission's table [1] (2023 figures include specials, added in Feb 2024):

| Election | Day 1 (Mon) | Day 2 | Day 3 | **Day 4 (Thu)** | Total advance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2017 | 39,570 | 48,238 | 66,084 | **72,932** | 1,240,740 |
| 2020 (COVID-boosted; day 4 = Mon 5 Oct) | — | — | — | **110,424** | 1,976,996 |
| 2023 | 65,845 | 80,899 | 88,671 | **103,617** | 1,640,204 |

The figures reported live in 2023 were lower than the later table. Day 1 was 56,557 live vs 65,845 in the table [2], and days 1–3 were 204,191 live vs 235,415 [3]. That puts live ordinary votes at about 86–87% of the table. **2023 day 4 as it would have been displayed live: about 89–90k.** The reference class (2017/2023 day 4, live basis) spans roughly 63k–90k, with 2020's ~110k as an enthusiasm/COVID outlier.

### Inside view

- **Enrolment law change (cut-off 25 Oct 2026).** In 2023 about 450k people enrolled during the voting period, and many of them cast advance *special* votes. In 2026 those people must already be on the roll, so they cast ordinary votes instead, or don't vote. This lifts the ordinary share and shrinks the gap between ordinary-only and combined figures. Net effect on the displayed number: roughly neutral to slightly positive.
- **Roll size.** 3.64M were enrolled before voting opened [4], a larger pool of ordinary-eligible voters than at the same point in 2023. Population growth since 2023 adds a few percent.
- **Calendar.** Monday 26 October is Labour Day. That may shift some day-1 voting, but has little effect on the Thursday. The long-run trend toward advance voting (2017 → 2023 [5]) leans up.
- **Displayed series.** If the 2026 page shows a combined ordinary + special figure from the start, the number moves toward the 2023 table basis (~104k).
- Central estimate: about 90k × 1.03 (roll/trend), with an allowance for combined display → **median 93k**. Tails are kept wide for turnout enthusiasm (2020-like → 115k+) and for a quieter campaign or fewer voting places (2017-like → <70k).

Percentiles: 5% 67k · 10% 74k · 25% 84k · 50% 93k · 75% 103k · 90% 115k · 95% 125k.

### What would change my mind

- The day 1–3 counts for 2026, which will exist before resolution but not before this forecast. A day 3 above ~100k would point to a day 4 of 110k+.
- Whether the 2026 live table reports ordinary votes only or combined votes issued (a ~±10% level shift).
