## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

YES if Zelenskyy is physically in Brussels and takes part in the Special European Council on 26 or 27 November 2026 (Brussels time). Doorsteps, the family photo or bilaterals at the summit venue count. Council newsroom material or credible reporting is enough to confirm it. NO covers a video link, a pre-recorded address, no participation, or no evidence of in-person presence. The question is annulled if the summit is postponed past 27 November or held all-virtual. Arithmetic: the summit opens 10 days after this forecast. The adjacent Euro Summit (26 Nov) and EU–Western Balkans summit (25 Nov) do not count unless he is at the EUCO itself. As of 16 Nov no invitation or format had been announced. In past cycles the Costa invitation letter arrived only days before the meeting.

### Outside view

The reference class is European Council meetings since early 2025 where Ukraine was on the agenda. His format, from scout digests built mostly on snippets:

- **In person (5):** special EUCO 6 Mar 2025, Oct 2025, Dec 2025, Jun 2026, Oct 2026.
- **Video (2–3):** Jun 2025, Mar 2026 (likely), informal Cyprus summit Apr 2026.

That is about 5 of 8, or ~60–65%. For the period with the most stakes (Dec 2025 to Oct 2026), regular summits went 3 of 4 in person. Special summits are mixed. He attended the Mar 2025 defence special in person. My unverified recollection is that he joined the Feb 2024 special on the €50bn Ukraine Facility by video.

### Inside view

- **For (+8 pp):** Ukraine's 2027 financing (~€45bn of new money, plus release of €45bn of the Support Loan) is the summit's central Ukraine item. Costa and von der Leyen confirmed this after meeting him on 12 Nov [1]. He lobbied in person on the same issue in October [2]. The pattern is that he travels when money is decided (the reparations-loan summit in Dec 2025, Oct 2026).
- **Against (−8 pp):** this special summit is largely an MFF negotiation among the 27 [3]. A non-member head of state usually gets only a short slot, which is easy to do by video, as in Feb 2024. He also made his pitch to Costa and von der Leyen in Dublin just two weeks earlier.
- **Against (−5 pp):** diplomacy is intense. Trilateral talks were held 10–11 Nov, more were possible the following week, and Witkoff and Kushner are shuttling [4]. His late-October Brussels trip was cancelled at short notice because US plans changed [5]. These are the disruption risks.

Net: about 50%, shaded slightly below because a lack of evidence resolves NO.

### Evidence

- 12 Nov 2026: joint statement by Costa and von der Leyen after meeting Zelenskyy in Dublin. The 26–27 Nov EUCO "will discuss Ukraine's urgent financing needs for 2027" [1].
- 15–16 Oct 2026: Zelenskyy in Brussels in person, asking for €70bn+ for 2027 [2].
- 21 Oct 2026: the special meeting is "dedicated to the MFF and funding for Ukraine". The report does not mention Zelenskyy [3].
- 10–11 Nov 2026: US–Ukraine–Russia talks in Abu Dhabi called constructive; more possible the week of 16 Nov [4].
- 26 Oct 2026: planned Brussels visit cancelled after a change in US plans [5].
- Dec 2025: pre-summit reports said he would join by video. He then attended in person [6]. Pre-summit format signals are noisy.

### What would change my mind

The main signal would be a Costa invitation letter or an EU official briefing on his format in the days before 26 Nov. A large swing factor would be a peace-talks round or a Washington meeting scheduled for 25–27 Nov.
