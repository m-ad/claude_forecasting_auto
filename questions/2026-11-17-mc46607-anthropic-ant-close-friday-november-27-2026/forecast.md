## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-17

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the official Nasdaq closing price in USD of Anthropic Class A (ANT) for the shortened session of Fri 27 Nov 2026 (1:00 p.m. ET early close), as shown in the Yahoo Finance history "Close" column, with Nasdaq historical quotes as the fallback. If ANT is not trading by then, the question is annulled, so the forecast is conditional on listing. Arithmetic: pricing is expected Wed 18 Nov and first trade Thu 19 Nov. 27 Nov is then the **6th** trading session: 19, 20, 23, 24 and 25 Nov, with 26 Nov a holiday, then 27 Nov. The range is $70–78. The range top plus a SpaceX-like +19% day-1 pop gives ~$93. The question's range is $40–170, open on both sides.

### Outside view

Reference class: very large, heavily oversubscribed tech IPOs. Day-1 closes vs offer price: Arm +25%, Alibaba +38%, Rivian +29%, SpaceX 2026 +19% (4x oversubscribed, 4% float). After a week the picture splits. Arm was back near its IPO price within days. Facebook was −33% at two weeks. SpaceX was +37% at one week and below its issue price within weeks. Typical outcome one week in: offer price +0–30%, with a dispersion of about ±20%. Assuming pricing at ~$78–82 (above-range talk, 6x book), the outside-view median is ~$92 and the 10–90% band is ~$70–120.

### Inside view

The best direct anchor is the pre-IPO perpetual future on Hyperliquid (trade.xyz `xyz:ANT`), which tracks the per-share price. It was $138 at launch on 31 Oct, $111 on 1 Nov, $117 on 5 Nov and $103.93 on 11 Nov. A fetch on 17 Nov showed $96.15 (−5.3% on the day). For SpaceX these perps ran ~7% above the day-1 close and 15–18% above the open, so the perp is a biased-high anchor: about $96 → ~$90–95 fair value.

Polymarket's IPO-day closing market-cap brackets have their mode at $1.25–1.5T (40.8%) and put 25.1% below $1.25T. On ~13.09bn shares outstanding that is ~$95–115 per share; on a larger share count it would be lower. This corroborates a day-1 close in the $90s–100s.

From day 1 to 27 Nov comes the usual post-pop fade (SpaceX, Arm). Underwriter stabilisation (the greenshoe) partly floors the price near the offer price. The AI tape is fragile: Nvidia has had six weekly declines and reports earnings on 17 Nov after the close, the evening before pricing.

Net: median $94, IQR $83–106, with fat tails. Below the offer price is possible (p10 $72); a squeeze from the small ~6–7% float is possible on the upside (p95 $130).
