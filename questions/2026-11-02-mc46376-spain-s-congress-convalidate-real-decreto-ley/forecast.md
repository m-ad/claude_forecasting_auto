## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-02

### What resolves YES

YES only if the Congreso's Diputación Permanente votes to convalidate Real Decreto-ley 29/2026 (the broad "función social de la vivienda" decree: eviction suspension for vulnerable households to 31 Dec 2030, limits on fund purchases, tax measures) **before 00:00 CET on 13 November 2026**, with yes votes outnumbering no votes. A tie, a lost vote, or no vote by then (including a session moved to 13 November) resolves NO. RDL 28/2026, the rental-extension decree, is voted separately and does not count. The arithmetic: 69 voting members, PP 27 + Vox 6 + Junts 1 = 34 against; PSOE 24 (+ Armengol = 25) + Sumar 6 + ERC 1 + Bildu 1 + PNV 1 + Podemos 1 = 35 for [2][3]. The margin is **one vote**: a single absence or defection on the government side with no matching loss on the other side gives a 34-34 tie, and the decree fails. A Junts abstention would widen the margin to two. Timing: the RDL was published 7 Oct. Thirty business days run to 12 Nov (Expansión/La República [6]) or 13 Nov (elriu.cat, which skips Sundays and the 12 Oct, 2 Nov and 9 Nov holidays [5]). The campaign opens 13 Nov, so **12 Nov is the only viable date**, and that is the date the government has given [1][6].

### Outside view

Reference class: decree-laws put to the Diputación Permanente after the Cortes are dissolved. Newtral counts 41 such decrees convalidated, and neither I nor the scouts found a single rejection [8]. In 2019 the Diputación Permanente convalidated 7 after dissolution, and RDL 24/2026 was convalidated in October 2026. That gives a base rate of roughly 95%+. The class is biased, though: governments normally hold a comfortable majority in that body, and here the margin is one seat. So I start at about 95% and adjust for how thin the margin is.

### Inside view

- **All six "yes" groups already voted for this text.** In the 2 Oct plenary, the predecessor RDL 26/2026 lost 172-178. The 172 included PSOE, Sumar, ERC, Bildu, PNV and Podemos [7][9]. PNV (spokesperson Maribel Vaquero) confirmed on 6 Oct that it will vote yes on this decree in the Diputación Permanente and no on the rental one [3][4]. It calls the decree "un parche" but supports it. I found no reversal through 2 Nov.
- **Absence risk is reduced by substitutes.** Each group has substitute members who stand in for absent titular members [2]. The single-seat groups (ERC, Bildu, PNV, Podemos) can therefore still vote if their titular member is away. Whether telematic voting is allowed in the Diputación Permanente is unverified.
- **Political strain from the Urda crash** (28 Oct, 69 dead). Sumar and PNV have demanded Transport Minister Óscar Puente's resignation [7]. But voting down an anti-eviction shield two weeks before an election, the day after a housing general strike, would hurt the left allies with their own voters. Using it as leverage over Puente is possible but not credible as a final vote. *Speculation*: this is a small risk.
- **Timing risk.** As of the latest reports the session is "prevista" (planned), not formally convened [1][6]. The government has every incentive to hold it, and 12 Nov is the last usable day before the campaign. Slipping to 13 Nov or later would resolve NO. I put that at about 3%.
- **Legal challenge** (claims of "fraude de ley" because the text was re-approved almost unchanged) would come after the vote, at the Constitutional Court [8]. It does not block the vote itself.

Decomposition: P(vote held by 12 Nov) ≈ 0.96 × P(yes > no | held) ≈ 0.90 → **≈ 0.86**. The 10% failure risk once the vote is held covers a PNV reversal (~3%), a left-ally protest vote or abstention (~3%), an uncovered absence or procedural error (~2%), and counting surprises such as a wrong seat tally or Armengol's vote being treated differently (~2%).

### Evidence

- 6 Oct 2026: PNV confirms it will save the anti-eviction decree in the Diputación Permanente [3][4].
- 16–27 Oct: EFE, Público and elDiario report the vote planned for 12 Nov, the day after the 11 Nov general strike; not formally convened [1].
- 21 Oct: La República/Expansión say 12 Nov is the deadline, and that PNV backed the first decree in the 2 Oct plenary as well [6].
- 2 Nov (updated): elriu.cat says the deadline is 13 Nov and the Mesa had not fixed a date [5].
- Late Oct: Puente appears before the Diputación Permanente on 3 Nov. Sumar and PNV want him to resign [7].
- Junts (Nogueras) objects to the procedure but admits it is legal. Its one seat is already counted as a no [10].

### What would change my mind

- PNV or any left ally (Podemos, ERC, Bildu, Sumar) stating it will abstain on or vote against RDL 29, for example by tying its vote to Puente's dismissal. That would move this below 40%.
- The Mesa failing to convene the session for 12 Nov, or scheduling it for 13 Nov or later. That would push it to about 5%.
