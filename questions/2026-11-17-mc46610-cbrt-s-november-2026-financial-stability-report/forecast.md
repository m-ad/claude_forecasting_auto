## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-17

### What resolves YES

The English FSR 2026-II, due 27 Nov 2026 (later is fine; no edition by 31 Dec → annulled), must list in its table of contents at least one numbered box whose **title** explicitly refers to the liquidation/winding-up of investment funds, to the SPK's September 2026 action, or to the funds placed in liquidation. A box titled about investment funds in general ("Recent Developments in Investment Funds") does **not** count, and neither does coverage in body text or inside a box with another title. So this needs two things: (a) a box on the fund episode at all, and (b) a title that names the liquidation. Arithmetic: P(YES) ≈ P(fund box) × P(title names liquidation | box) ≈ 0.8 × 0.3 ≈ 0.24.

### Outside view

CBRT FSR box titles are thematic, not event-named. The November 2025 FSR had six boxes, all generic, including Box III.1.I "Recent Developments in Investment Funds" [5]. The May 2025 FSR covered the March 2025 market turmoil in body text, and I found no box named after it [6]. Base rate for an event-named box title: low, maybe 10–20 % even when a major domestic shock occurred.

### Inside view

- **For a box on funds (strong):** this was the largest fund crisis in Turkish capital-markets history: 131 funds, about TRY 0.9–1 trillion, a Financial Stability Committee meeting and CBRT liquidity measures [2][3]. Investment funds are already a standing FSR theme (Nov 2025 box). A box on funds in FSR 2026-II looks about 80 % likely.
- **Against the title naming the liquidation (strongest single datum):** five days ago, on 12 Nov 2026, the CBRT's Inflation Report 2026-IV dealt with exactly this episode in **Kutu 2.2 "Yatırım Fonlarında Son Dönem Gelişmeler"** ("Recent Developments in Investment Funds"). That is a generic title that would resolve NO here [1]. The official line from the CBRT and the FSC is containment ("temporary and manageable"), which favours neutral wording.
- **For it:** the CBRT's own research blog (4 Nov) titled its piece "Fund Liquidations and the Real Sector" [4], so the institution does use the word. An FSR box on liquidation-specific risks (e.g. the real-sector or bank exposures to funds in liquidation) could carry it.
- Net: P(title names it | box) ≈ 0.30, giving ≈ 0.24.

### Evidence

- Inflation Report 2026-IV ToC (PDF read 2026-11-17): Kutu 2.2 "Yatırım Fonlarında Son Dönem Gelişmeler", p. 42 [1].
- FSC, 17 Sep 2026: problems in funds of a limited number of portfolio managers, "temporary and manageable"; TRY 110.8bn net fund outflows on 15–16 Sep [3].
- SPK, 17 Sep 2026: 131 funds of 7 managers ordered liquidated; window extended to 6 months on 20 Sep [2]. By 22 Oct, 66 funds (TRY 40.8bn) had been paid out (secondary source, scout).
- CBRT blog, 4 Nov 2026: about 3,000 firms, TRY 116bn exposure, limited impact [4].
- Karahan (6 Oct, 12 Nov): impact contained; outflows mostly went to deposits [7].
- Nov 2025 FSR box titles: generic, including "Recent developments in investment funds" (search snippet of the ToC; the PDF returned 404) [5].
