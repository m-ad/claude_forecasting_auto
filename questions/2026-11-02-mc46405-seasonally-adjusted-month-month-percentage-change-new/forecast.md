## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-02

### What resolves YES

The number is the seasonally adjusted month-on-month % change in the **total** (all industries, including services and other non-retail) electronic card spending value for October 2026 versus September 2026, as first published by Stats NZ on 12 Nov 2026 (10:45 NZDT) in the sentence "the total value of electronic card spending, including the two non-retail categories … [increased/decreased] from September 2026, up/down $X million (Y percent)". Later revisions do not count. Last published period: September 2026 (release 16 Oct 2026), total −0.5% (−$51m to ~$9.2bn actual); target period is October 2026, one month later, no gap. Note the retail-only headline (Sep: −0.4%) and Trading Economics' headline (−0.4%) are a different series from the total (−0.5%); the total is what resolves. Reported to 1 decimal. Fallback clause is not relevant (Stats NZ always states the total). Typical monthly size in 2026: |move| about 0.5–2.2 pp.

### Outside view

Reference class: first-published SA total m/m changes. In 2024–25 these were small (Aug 24 −0.2, Sep 24 +0.3, Oct 24 +0.4, Nov 24 −0.1, Jan 25 0.0, Apr 25 −0.2, Aug 25 +0.4, Sep 25 −0.4, Nov 25 +1.9, Dec 25 −1.0): sd roughly 0.6–0.7, mean near 0. In 2026 volatility is much higher (Jan −0.7, Apr −1.6, May +2.2, Jul +1.0, Aug −0.5, Sep −0.5), sd ~1.2 on the known months (n=6, selected months only). Prior: centred ~0 to +0.2 (nominal growth ~ +0.2/month trend, annual growth only +0.9% y/y), sd ~0.9.

### Inside view

Adjustments from a ~+0.1 prior (counted once each):
- Petrol: MBIE weekly regular-petrol price averaged ~352 c/L in October (4 weeks to 23 Oct) against ~312 in September (+13%), and stayed elevated at 357 on 23 Oct. Fuel is ~5–6% of total card value, so nominal fuel spending adds roughly +0.2 pp even with falling volumes (+0.2 pp).
- Two successive −0.5% months after a +1.0% July make partial mean reversion likely (+0.2 pp). Westpac had forecast +0.8% for September's retail figure and got −0.4%.
- Offsetting, not adjusted for separately: consumer confidence (ANZ-Roy Morgan 30 Oct) fell, current conditions 87.3 → 81.1, "cost of living pressures dented spending plans"; Westpac expects volumes "subdued in the near term". These keep the median from going higher and widen the downside.

Median +0.4%, IQR [−0.3, +1.0] (p5 −1.5, p95 +2.1; widened after the tail-case scout found 2026 retail/total moves exceeded |1.0| in roughly half of months and that big falls were each followed by rebounds of +1.3 to +1.7, but Aug/Sep small falls were not); wide tails because 2026 has seen −1.6 and +2.2 moves and seasonal adjustment around the five-Saturday month/Labour Day (26 Oct) is unpredictable. Stats NZ rounds to one decimal.
