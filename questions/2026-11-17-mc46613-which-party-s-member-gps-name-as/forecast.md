## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-17

### What resolves YES

The option is decided by the party membership of the person Gabungan Parti Sarawak (GPS) itself names for N53 Bawang Assan. GPS naming means Abang Johari's announcement or the appointment letters (watikah); a component party claiming the seat on its own does not count. The naming must be public before 16:00 UTC on 27 Nov 2026 (00:00 Malaysia time on nomination day, 28 Nov). If GPS replaces its candidate before the cutoff, the most recent naming counts. If nothing is named in time, the answer is "No GPS candidate…".

Timing arithmetic: Abang Johari said around 15 Nov that GPS will announce all 82 candidates on **25 Nov** [5]. That leaves about 2 days of slack before the cutoff, so the "none named" option needs a slip or a deliberate hold-back of this one seat. A candidate who is not a member of SUPP or PDP (from PBB or PRS, or a neutral pick) falls into option 3.

### Outside view

There is no clean reference class. The closest is GPS/BN seat disputes in which an incumbent from the coalition (or a defector to it) is set against a component party's "traditional" claim. BN/GPS usually settles these with the incumbent's party keeping the seat, or with a face-saving fresh face from that party. In 2016 BN created "direct candidates" so that Wong and his allies could keep their seats against SUPP. That gives a prior of roughly 55/45 in favour of the incumbent's party (PDP) over the claimant (SUPP), with a few percent for a third option or a delay.

### Inside view

- **For PDP:** Wong holds the seat. Joshua Ting, PDP's Bawang Assan Youth chief, is Abang Johari's political secretary and is widely reported as PDP's pick, which also answers Sim's "fresh face" demand. Wong and PDP president Tiong King Sing met the Premier about the seat in late October [3, 4]. One possible package deal is that SUPP takes Batu Lintang through incumbent See Chee How, who has applied to join SUPP [2], while PDP keeps Bawang Assan. That trade is speculation.
- **For SUPP:** SUPP held the seat for five terms before 2016, and its GPS candidate ran in 2021. The PBB branch backs SUPP. Analysts in 2024 expected SUPP to prevail [6]. Kevin Lau has nursed the seat since 2024 and was formally proposed in July 2026 [1].
- **Neither:** There is a small chance of a neutral or PBB compromise candidate (option 3). A slip past the cutoff ("none named") is unlikely given the 25 Nov date.

Net: PDP 0.50, SUPP 0.40, PBB/PRS/other 0.04, none named 0.06.

### What would change my mind

The Batu Lintang outcome is the key signal. If See Chee How is admitted to SUPP and keeps that seat, the trade logic favours PDP in Bawang Assan. If he is not, SUPP's claim to Bawang Assan gets stronger. A report that Ting has taken pre-nomination candidate photos, or that Wong has been told to retire with no PDP successor, would also move the forecast.
