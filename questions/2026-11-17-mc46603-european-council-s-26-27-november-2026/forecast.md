## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-17

### What resolves YES

YES if any text the Council publishes as conclusions, or as a statement of heads of state or government, for the special European Council of 26–27 November 2026 contains the ordinal "22nd"/"twenty-second" applied to a sanctions/restrictive-measures package against Russia. That includes MFF conclusions, a separate Ukraine text, annexes, and a text "firmly supported by 26". Generic wording ("a new sanctions package", "further sanctions") resolves NO, as does no published text at all. The question rests on three things: (a) the Commission tables the package before or at the summit (von der Leyen said "by 26 November"; Kallas on 16 Nov said "in the coming days"); (b) the summit, called for the MFF and Ukraine's 2027 financing, issues a Ukraine text; (c) that text has a sanctions paragraph that names the package by number.

### Outside view

Regular European Councils name the pending or just-adopted package by number: June 2026 ("swift adoption of the 21st"), October 2026 ("welcome… the 21st"), and EUCO 2/26 (25 leaders, "swift adoption of the 20th") [1][7]. The number is dropped when no proposal exists yet: December 2025 said "a new sanctions package… after its presentation in early 2026" [8]. Special summits are narrower. Neither the 1 February 2024 special (MFF and Ukraine) nor the 6 March 2025 special (defence and Ukraine) named a package by number; both used "new sanctions" or nothing [5][6]. The base rate is about 70–80% for regular summits with a package on the table, and about 0/2 for special summits. That puts the prior near 35–45%.

### Inside view

- The package is very likely tabled before the summit: von der Leyen said "by 26 November"; Kallas on 16 Nov said "coming days"; Rikard Jozwiak (RFE/RL) reports a target of adoption by Christmas [2][3]. Commission delays are common, so I put this at about 75%.
- The summit does have a Ukraine item (2027 financing, a gap of about €70bn) [4]. A Ukraine text is very likely (~85%), possibly backed by only 26 leaders, which still counts.
- The risk is a financing-only Ukraine text, with sanctions left to the regular 17–18 December EUCO. That is where an adoption call would fit naturally. The Costa–von der Leyen joint statement ties the 22nd package to the summit "in parallel", which is a mild argument for a sanctions line.
- Rough model: 0.85 × (0.75 × 0.55 + 0.25 × 0.25) ≈ 0.40. My forecast is 40%.

### Evidence

- 2026-11-12: Costa and von der Leyen joint statement: "The Commission will in parallel present its 22nd package of sanctions against Russia" [3].
- 2026-11-16: Kallas after the Foreign Affairs Council: 22nd package "in the coming days". Negotiations with member states start this week [2].
- 2026-11-16: separate listings of 12 individuals and 101 entities, explicitly not the 22nd package [9].
- 2026-11-10/11: Costa's reply to Merz: the summit agenda is the MFF and Ukraine's 2027 needs [4].
