Model note (verbatim):
"This Guesstimate model provides an estimation of the impact of our Humane Slaughter Initiative.
It is based on data from signed MOUs and aims to estimate the proportion of shrimp stunned across
our partner producers... the data presented in this model is not linked to individual producers,
in order to protect the confidentiality of each producer's business information."

Derived (own calculation):
  sum(A_i * B_i) = 86,624 MT  ~ matches SWP's published "86,875 MT" committed
  86,875 MT / 14 g  = 6.20 bn  -> published headline 6.3 bn
  86,875 MT / 20 g  = 4.34 bn  -> published low  4.3 bn
  86,875 MT / 10 g  = 8.69 bn  -> published high 8.7 bn
  => headline = committed MT converted at 14 g/shrimp (71,429 shrimp/MT); range is the g/shrimp range.
  Mean committed volume per agreement = 86,875 / 35 = 2,482 MT = ~0.177 bn shrimp per agreement.
  Coverage of partners' own total volume = 86,624 / 410,546 = 21.1% (headroom without new MOUs).
