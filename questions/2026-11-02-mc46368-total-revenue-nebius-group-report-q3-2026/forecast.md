## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-02

### What resolves YES

This resolves to the "Revenues" line for the three months ended 30 September 2026 in Nebius Group's Q3 2026 earnings release, in US$ millions, as first published. The release is scheduled before the US open on 12 November 2026 at nebius.com/investor-hub. The last reported quarter is Q2 2026, the three months to 30 June 2026: $582.3M group revenue, of which $574.9M was AI cloud [1]. The target is the very next quarter, July to September 2026, so this is a one-quarter-ahead forecast. The question range is 700 to 1,150 with both bounds open.

The arithmetic that matters is annualized run-rate revenue (ARR), which Nebius defines as last-month AI cloud revenue × 12 [1]:
- ARR was $3.0B at end-June, so June AI cloud revenue was about $250M.
- If revenue had stayed flat at that level, Q3 would be about $750M plus roughly $7M of non-cloud revenue.
- Hitting the ~$905–935M consensus therefore needs about 9–12% monthly growth, or a September exit ARR near $4.4–4.6B.
- That exit ARR sits on a straight line from $3.0B to the $7–9B year-end ARR guide.
- Full-year guidance of $3.0–3.4B less the $981.3M booked in H1 leaves $2.02–2.42B for H2. A Q3 of $920M then implies a Q4 of $1.10–1.50B. Consensus Q4 is ~$1.45B, near the top of guidance.

### Outside view

The reference class is Nebius's own revenue surprise against sell-side consensus, a one-quarter-ahead forecast made about 10 days before the print. The record over Q2 2025 to Q2 2026 is roughly +4/+10%, −6%, −2/−7%, +3/+6% and +1.5/+7%, depending on the data provider [5]. That gives a mean near 0 to +2% and a spread (standard deviation) of about 5–6%. The two misses came in H2 2025 and were caused by capacity-timing slips at colocation partners [5][8].

Consensus for this quarter is $899.9M (BofA's cited figure, 29 Oct) [4], $903.5–904.8M (Benzinga and Equibles, 17 analysts, 18 Oct) [2][3] and up to ~$937–952M on some providers [3]. The centre is about $910M, the analyst range $877–943M. The outside view is therefore centred near $910M with a standard deviation of about $50M.

### Inside view

- **Upward pull:** Nebius beat consensus in the last two quarters, the two before them having been misses.
- **Firm base:** All Microsoft tranches were delivered, two of them in July–August [1]. Management said capacity deployed late in Q2 would contribute in Q3 [6]. The company has repeatedly been sold out of capacity [6]. Together these make a large Q3 step-up from the June run-rate fairly firm.
- **No Q3 upside from big deals:** The Meta $12–27B deal, the Q2 landmark deals and the Q3 short-term deal all go live in Q4 2026 or 2027 [1], so they add little to Q3.
- **Downside:** The main risk is a slip in activating capacity inside the quarter, which is the cause of the earlier misses. The Vineland delay scare (DA Davidson, August) mainly affects Q4 and 2027 [8].
- **Sell-off is not a revenue signal:** The October sector sell-off was about financing, not Nebius revenue, and the consensus barely moved ($907.6M on 18 Sep vs $904.8M on 18 Oct) [2].

I nudge the median to $920M, about +1% on the consensus centre and below Equibles' own $934.5M. Tails are slightly fat on both sides: the downside is a capacity slip, the upside Equibles' 16%-per-month scenario at ~$1,024M [2]. The 5–95% range is 825–1,015.
