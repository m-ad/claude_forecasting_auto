## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-03

### What resolves YES

By 2026-11-13 23:59 UTC, a *standalone paper-style document* (arXiv preprint listing both Alpöge and Sussman, or a PDF on a personal/institutional web page of either, with title, both authors, and an abstract/introduction stating a theorem) asserting existence of four-dimensional pure (SU(2)) Yang–Mills theory must be publicly accessible. Files in code repositories, READMEs, notes, slides and social media do not count. So the existing 153-page "zeroth draft" PDF in the GitHub repo does **not** count, and neither does any 3D (YM3/QED3/QCD3) document. Window: 10 days (Nov 3 → Nov 13). Status today: Sussman's site lists no Yang–Mills preprint (only a link to the GitHub repo, under "works in progress… to be updated as projects near completion"); no arXiv listing for the pair. Gap to YES: a new, human-presentable 4D manuscript that does not yet exist, or relocation of a repo draft to a personal web page with an abstract.

### Outside view

Reference class: authors with a long, unvetted, mostly-AI-written claimed proof of a major problem, in a public priority race, who have said they are working in a repo. A priority race raises the chance of rushing a preprint, but short-window "will X post a paper" events that have not been announced mostly do not happen: I put a generic prior around 10–12% for a rushed posting within 10 days (speculative; no n-sized dataset fits — I found no precedent of a long unvetted constructive-QFT manuscript being rushed to arXiv after a scoop).

### Inside view

Strong evidence for the authors' own intention to NOT rush (README, fetched 2026-11-03): Alpöge: the 4D argument is "extremely long and basically not understandable as is… we need to not incentivize rushing out 10,000-page slop"; Sussman: "Dropping 1000+ pages of slop and declaring it a finished business would be inimical… proceed in a collaborative fashion, at a human pace"; after OpenAI's announcement: "we will continue working." Recent commits (Nov 2) are 3D/QED3/QCD3 material, not a 4D paper. YM4 is labelled "unfinished". Sussman told Scientific American they were "maybe even as close as a few weeks away" (vague, second-hand, not a posting date). OpenAI's own write-up is withheld, lowering the priority pressure to post something immediately. Even if they chose to post, a personal-site PDF is possible without arXiv moderation, and the mere relabelling of the repo draft with abstract on Sussman's site could technically qualify — that is the main YES path (~3–4%), plus a quick arXiv rush (~2%). Net: about 5%. Adjustment from prior: −7 pp, one event (README stance and recent activity).
