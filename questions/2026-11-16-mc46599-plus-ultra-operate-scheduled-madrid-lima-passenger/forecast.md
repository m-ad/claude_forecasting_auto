## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

YES if a regularly scheduled Plus Ultra MAD→LIM passenger flight (PU301 or another PU number on the route) actually departs with a scheduled Madrid-local departure date of Thursday 26 November 2026. Flightradar24 PU301 history is the source, with Airportia or credible reports as fallbacks. Diversions after takeoff still count. Cancellations, removal from the schedule, a route suspension that is already in force, and charters all resolve NO. Arithmetic: sales stopped on 13 Nov, so 26 Nov is 13 days later and 10 days after today (16 Nov). Thursday is a normal Lima day (daily except Tuesday). The 13 Nov FR24 listing ran only to 20 Nov, which looks like FR24's usual ~7-day schedule horizon and is not evidence of a cut-off.

### Outside view

There is no clean reference class. A carrier that is still flying and exits a route usually announces a last-flight date and keeps carrying booked passengers for days to weeks. An airline that collapses outright stops all flights at once (Spirit, Flybe, Eastern Airways, Lynx). Plus Ultra sits in between: it is in preconcurso (pre-insolvency negotiations with creditors) but still flying. My rough prior that a route which has stopped selling, with no end date announced, is still operating 13 days later is ~45–50%.

### Inside view

Points toward NO:
- The airline plans to cut more than 61% of its flight hours, and its liquidity is acute.
- El Comercio writes that the Lima routes "han cancelado".
- Concentrating on Venezuela frees aircraft and cuts fuel spend at once.
- Even if the route continues, a distressed carrier may first consolidate the remaining Lima frequencies down to a few days a week, which puts any single Thursday at risk.

Points toward YES:
- No last-flight date had been set as of 13 Nov.
- Rerouting Lima passengers is costly: there is no partner network, so the airline would have to buy seats on other carriers or issue refunds.
- The airline says it wants to "protect continuity".
- A reader report says Buenos Aires flights were still scheduled for 18 Nov.

Net: 38%, with a wide IQR because I could not check the resolution source (FR24 and Airportia both returned 403).
