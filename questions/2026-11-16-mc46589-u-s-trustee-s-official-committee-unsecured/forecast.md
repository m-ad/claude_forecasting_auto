## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

The question resolves YES only if the U.S. Trustee files a notice of appointment of an official committee of unsecured creditors in In re The Children's Place, Inc., Case No. 26-22682 (SLM) (Bankr. D.N.J.), with a docket filing date on or before Nov 27, 2026, and the most recent such notice names **seven or more** members. Counsel and advisers don't count. If there is no notice, or the notice names six or fewer, it resolves NO. Timing: the petition was filed Nov 8. Nov 26 is Thanksgiving, so in practice the notice has to be filed by **Nov 25 or Nov 27, about day 17–19 of the case**. No committee existed through docket no. 110 (Nov 12, day 4). Today is day 8. So YES needs two things: an appointment within about the next 9–11 days, **and** a size of at least 7. P(YES) = P(appointed in time) × P(≥7 | appointed).

### Outside view

- **Time to appointment.** In large retail Chapter 11s the U.S. Trustee usually appoints a committee 11–16 days after filing: Rite Aid 2025 (D.N.J.) took 11 days (May 5 → May 16), Joann 2025 13 days, Big Lots 2024 about 14 days, and Party City 2024 16 days (over the holidays) [5][6][7][8]. The deadline here is about day 17. The base rate for an appointment in time is roughly 85–90%, and nearly every retailer with more than $90M of trade debt gets a committee.
- **Committee size.** U.S. Trustee guidance says a committee "ordinarily consists of" the holders of the seven largest unsecured claims who are willing to serve [9]. Examples from large retail and apparel cases: Rite Aid 9 (twice, D.N.J.), Saks Global 10, Liberated Brands 7, Big Lots 7 (unverified), Gymboree 2017 7, Food52 7 reduced to 5 [5][7][9][10][11]. Mid-size liquidations often get 3–5 members. For a case with $881M of liabilities, P(≥7 | appointed) is roughly 45–55%.

### Inside view

- **Points to a larger committee:** the case is big (more than 10,000 creditors, $93.9M of trade debt in the top 30 list) [1][3]. A fast 363 sale process (sale of assets under section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code) has bids due Dec 9 and objections due Dec 8, which gives creditors a reason to get organised. Landlords and vendors are typically represented together, which pushes the count up.
- **Points to a smaller committee:** the top creditors are mostly foreign apparel manufacturers (Hansae in Korea, a Hong Kong entity, TNG in Vietnam), and in a liquidation fewer of them may be willing to serve. Simon and Brookfield are investors in Catalyst, a possible bidder [4], which could keep major landlords off the committee or make them recuse themselves. The case is a wind-down with a lender-funded DIP loan (debtor-in-possession financing) and probably a small recovery for unsecured creditors, which reduces willingness to serve.
- **Timing:** no appointment had been reported publicly by Nov 16 (searched news, the Omni docket returned 403). That is normal on day 8. I put P(appointed by Nov 25–27) at about 0.85.
- **Arithmetic:** 0.85 × 0.48 ≈ 0.41.

### Evidence

- The Children's Place filed Chapter 11 in D.N.J. on Nov 8, 2026 with more than 10,000 creditors, about $94M owed to the top 30, bids due Dec 9 and nine proposals received [1][2][3].
- Catalyst Brands is bidding for about 200 stores and the IP. Simon and Brookfield are Catalyst investors [4].
- As of Nov 16, 2026, no news report, Law360 brief or U.S. Trustee solicitation mentions a committee for this case (searches on 2026-11-16).
- Comparable committees: Rite Aid 2025 had 9 members, appointed after 11 days [5]. Saks Global had 10 [10]. Liberated Brands had 7 [11]. Food52 went from 7 to 5 [11]. Gymboree 2017 had 7 (Hansoll, Li & Fung, Simon, PREIT, GGP and others) [12].

### What would change my mind

- An early notice of appointment, or a reported formation meeting, would push P(appointed) to about 1 and leave only the size question.
- The size is the main driver. A report that the U.S. Trustee's solicitation drew few volunteers, or that landlords stayed out because of the Catalyst conflict, would point to 3–5 members. A committee that includes landlords together with the large Asian vendors would point to 7.
