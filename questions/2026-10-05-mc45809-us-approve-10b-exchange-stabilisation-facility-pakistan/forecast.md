## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-05

### What resolves YES

YES if, **before 2027-01-01**, credible sources confirm that the **US government has formally
approved** an exchange stabilisation support facility, or an equivalent currency swap, of **at
least $10 billion** for Pakistan. Fine print: the facility does not have to be executed or drawn
before 2027. Approval is what counts [1]. What does *not* count: a smaller facility ($3–8B); a
non-swap package (DFC refinery financing, EXIM aircraft loans, investment pledges); talks
"progressing"; an IMF or Gulf package. Grey zone: a Trump or Bessent announcement of a "$10B
swap framework", like the Argentina 9 Oct 2025 "framework agreement", would probably resolve YES
even before a signed agreement, because the Argentina precedent was widely reported as US approval.
I put weight on that lenient reading.

### Outside view

- **Exchange Stabilization Fund (ESF, the Treasury's emergency fund that lends to foreign
  governments without a vote in Congress) credit to foreign governments is rare.** Large cases
  in roughly 45 years: Mexico 1982, Mexico 1995 ($20B), Brazil 1998 (via the Bank for
  International Settlements), Uruguay 2002 ($1.5B bridge), Argentina 2025 ($20B swap) [2][3].
  That is about one large facility every 9–10 years. The annual rate that *any* given country
  gets one is far below 1%.
- **Conditional on a public request by a friendly government:** requests are not systematically
  logged. Many countries in distress (Turkey 2018, Sri Lanka 2022, Egypt 2023, Pakistan itself in
  2019 and 2023) went to the IMF, China or the Gulf rather than the ESF, and did not get US
  bilateral liquidity. I estimate that 10–25% of serious public requests to the US Treasury get
  approved within about 6 months. Argentina 2025 is the only recent success. It was driven by
  ideological alignment and an imminent election.
- **Time-scaling:** the request dates from July 2026 [4]. At most ~3 months of the window
  remain, out of a ~5–6-month decision horizon since the request.

Outside-view prior: **~8–12%**.

### Inside view

Chain to YES:
1. Treasury decides to offer *some* stabilisation facility before Dec 31: **~22%**. For: Trump's
   warm public praise of Shehbaz and Munir, Pakistan's role as US–Iran mediator, and the strategic
   aim of not leaving Islamabad dependent on China [5][6]. Against: Bessent's July readout praised
   reforms but stressed "self-reliance" and a return to markets, with no mention of the facility
   [7]. Pakistan is a long-run International Monetary Fund (IMF) client, under a $7B Extended Fund
   Facility. Former Treasury official Mark Sobel publicly urged a "no" [8]. Pakistan's own
   timelines have slipped: "by September" became "within two months" on 17 Sep [4][9].
2. Size ≥ $10B, given an offer: **~45%**. The ESF held only **$23.5B in Treasury securities and
   $4.3B in foreign currency** (Feb 2026), plus SDRs [3]. The $20B Argentina line is still formally
   open [10]. A $10B commitment would claim most of the remaining dollar liquidity, unless Treasury
   draws on SDRs or the Argentina line is cut. A scaled-down line ($3–5B) or a mix of
   DFC/EXIM/guarantees is the natural compromise. Aurangzeb said himself that this is "not about
   a credit line or a loan" but "a signal" [9], and a signal can be sent with less.
3. Formal approval reported before Jan 1, given 1 and 2: **~90%**.

0.22 × 0.45 × 0.90 ≈ **9%**.

A 5-year maturity would need a presidential determination of "unique or emergency circumstances",
notified to Congress, under the ESF six-month rule [3]. Argentina shows this is no real barrier,
but it adds some friction and public scrutiny (H.R. 6061 and Warren's letters on Argentina) [11][12].

### Evidence

- 2026-07-21/22: Reuters reports the request for a "Bilateral Exchange Stabilisation Support
  Facility" of $10B, up to 5 years, made to Bessent [4][13].
- 2026-07-22: Treasury readout of the Bessent–Aurangzeb meeting praises reforms and the push to
  return to capital markets, and does **not** mention the facility [7]. LR ≈ 0.8.
- 2026-07-24: economists sceptical; Sobel says Treasury should decline [8]. LR ≈ 0.9.
- 2026-08-13: Dawn analysis: "It may not be approved, and even an approved line may never be
  drawn" [6].
- 2026-08-19/26: Pakistan says the request is "under consideration" and discussions
  "constructive", with progress "by September" [9][14].
- 2026-09-17: Aurangzeb expects a US response "within two months" (≈ mid-November). He also
  wants a bigger China swap line and is talking to EXIM and DFC [15]. The timeline slip and the
  parallel China ask: LR ≈ 0.85. The EXIM/DFC tracks look like the more likely form of US support.
- 2026-09-22/23: Trump calls Shehbaz a "wonderful person"; a brief UNGA reception meeting; no
  economic deliverable announced [16][17]. LR ≈ 1.0.
- **Absent:** no leak from the US side (Treasury, NSC, Congress) that a swap is being drafted. In
  the Argentina case, Bessent signalled support publicly ("all options on the table") about three
  weeks before the framework [2]. Nothing similar exists here 2.5 months after the request.
  That absence is meaningful.
