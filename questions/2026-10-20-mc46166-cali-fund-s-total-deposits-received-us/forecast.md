## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-20

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the cumulative "Total Deposits" figure in US dollars for the Cali Fund (fund code CAL00), as shown on the UNDP Multi-Partner Trust Fund (MPTF) Office portal (mptf.undp.org/fund/cal00) when checked on or after 31 Oct 2026, covering the period through 31 Oct. If that line is missing, "Total Source of Funds" is used instead. Pledges that have not been paid do not count. The question's starting value, at the lower bound of the range, is $7,085 (mid-October 2026). Both bounds are open. Because the range is linear from $7,085 to $2,000,000, the first bin covers roughly $7,085 to $17,050.

### Outside view

Deposits have arrived in a thin trickle. There have been three contributors in about 20 months since the February 2025 launch: TierraViva AI paid $1,000 on 19 Nov 2025, Prozomix paid $5,000 in July 2026, and E-BIOM paid about $1,100 in October 2026 [2][3][4]. That is roughly one deposit every 4–8 months, and none above $5,000. Over an 11-day window, the base rate says the most likely outcome is no change at all, and that any change would be a few thousand dollars.

### Inside view

Two things push upward from the trickle rate. First, AFP reported on 20 Oct 2026 that "two others have pledged a further $3,571" [2]. If both are paid and booked by 31 Oct, the total reaches about $10,656. Second, COP17 (Yerevan, 19–30 Oct) is the natural venue for new announcements. The EU is co-hosting Cali Fund sessions and proposing a pilot phase [6]. But pledges usually come before transfers, and companies have been reluctant to go first. Ginkgo Bioworks announced an intention to contribute in 2025 and had still transferred nothing as of September 2026 [3]. AstraZeneca declined to act as a "frontrunner" [7]. I found no announced large deposit. Rough probabilities:
- about 55%: unchanged at about $7,085
- about 30%: small deposits bring it to roughly $8k–17k
- about 8%: $17k–100k
- about 4%: above $100k
- about 3–5% below $7,085, from reporting or FX differences, or a different figure being read

Some mass is deliberately placed just below the lower bound. The most likely outcome sits exactly on range_min, so this hedges against how a value equal to the boundary gets bucketed.

### Evidence

- 20 Oct 2026, AFP via Kuwait Times: the fund "so far it has collected only $7,085": TierraViva $1,000, Prozomix $5,000, E-BIOM $1,085. Two others have pledged $3,571 [2].
- 15 Oct 2026, Cali Fund newsletter: E-BIOM is the third contributor and committed to 0.1% of its eDNA service value annually from 2027. No COP17 contributors are named [4].
- 1 Sep 2026, Mongabay: as of August, two contributions totalling $6,000. Ginkgo had announced but not transferred [3].
- 12 May 2026, InfoGM: one contribution recorded ($1,000) [5].
- The MPTF portal could not be read live on 20 Oct 2026 because its values are JavaScript-rendered. The $7,085 starting value comes from the question text and matches AFP [1][2].
- IISD's COP17 explainer says there have been "virtually no contributions" to date [8].

### What would change my mind

The main upside risk is a COP17 announcement that a mid-size or large company (Ginkgo, or a pharma or seed firm) or a government has actually transferred money, not just pledged. Even then, the deposit has to be booked in UNDP's general ledger by 31 Oct. On the downside, if the $3,571 pledges were not paid within the window, the outcome would sit exactly at $7,085.
