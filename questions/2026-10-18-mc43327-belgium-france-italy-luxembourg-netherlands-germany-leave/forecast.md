## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-18

### What resolves YES

Resolves YES if, before 1 January 2027, Belgium, France, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands or Germany formally notifies the European Council of its intention to leave the EU under Article 50 (the question says "Treaty of Lisbon", meaning the Treaty on European Union). It also resolves YES if the EU has dissolved completely, or been replaced by a successor union under another name, by the same date. A referendum, a party pledge or a government threat does not count. Only the notification itself does.

### Outside view

No founding member has ever triggered Article 50. Only the UK has done so, once, in 2017, among about 28 members over roughly 15 years of the clause's existence. The window is about 10 weeks. Nothing is pending: no referendum has been called, and the process would need a government decision first.

### Inside view

Searches turned up no current report of any such move. A 2026 analysis says France's Rassemblement National no longer pledges a Frexit referendum. Germany's AfD only conditions a referendum on the failure of reform demands, and it is not in government. Italy and the Netherlands show no active exit process either. A notification in ten weeks would need a government that wants out, a prior vote or decision, and none of this is visible. EU dissolution by year-end is also negligible: a Polymarket mirror put it near 3% (about 97% No) in June 2026, which I treat as long-dated-market noise. I forecast about 0.3%. The 2% floor in the instructions is the only reason I do not go lower. I use 1.0% for the median to stay inside the allowed range and leave room for unseen shocks and for an LLM resolver misreading the criteria.

### Evidence

- Search on 2026-10-18 found no news of an Article 50 notice or an exit referendum in any of the six countries [1][2].
- RN has dropped its Frexit pledge; exit parties in Italy and France have minimal support [1].
- A Polymarket "any country withdraws before 2027" market showed about 6% (all EU members, mirror snapshot of 2026-06-29). This is likely inflated by the long lock-up of money [2].

### What would change my mind

A government of one of the six announcing a binding exit referendum or a formal notice before year-end. Nothing of the kind is visible now. The only other factor is how the criteria are read: I read "dissolved" literally, as the EU legally ceasing to exist.
