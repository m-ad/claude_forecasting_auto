### Inside view

Start at 10,250 and add a typical recent beat of ~$170M → ~10,420M. Supportive: the quarter was ~2.5 months in at guidance time with backlog visibility; DRAM/HBM packaging and leading-edge logic strong; Lam beat its own Sept-quarter midpoint by $120M (+1.5%) on 21 Oct and raised its 2026 WFE view to the mid-$150B range. Cautions: the guide was already a very large step (+12.5% q/q, ~$700M above pre-guide consensus), so some of the typical beat may already be "used"; Lam says supply-chain and cleanroom constraints are delaying shipments into the next quarter; China risk (28% of Q3 revenue; April 2026 Hua Hong shipment stop; no new October rule found); the "Other" segment guide ($510M vs $294M in Q3) is a large step that adds execution risk. The October equity selloff is sentiment about AI capex, not evidence of order cancellations within a quarter already largely shipped. I put the median slightly below midpoint+typical beat (10,420 vs ~10,450) and keep a fat lower tail. Single-event adjustment: the recurring beat pattern (+170M).
