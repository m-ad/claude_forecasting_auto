## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

YES only if a team identified as Russian ("Russia"/"RUS", "ROC", "AIN") appears in the participating-teams list at https://www.iihf.com/en/events/2027/ww18 **or** in at least one game at .../ww18/schedule, as those pages stand at 23:59 UTC on 26 Nov 2026. If the pages can't be loaded, the nearest earlier Wayback capture is used. News, statements and fixtures sent privately to federations do not count; "TBD" placeholders do not count. On 13 Nov both pages showed 8 teams (CAN, CZE, GER, HUN, SUI, SVK, SWE, USA) and 21 games, with no Russia. So the question is whether the IIHF **publicly** changes the Ostrava field within 10 days (16–26 Nov). The tournament starts 8 Jan, 53 days after today. The likely way in is a swap with Germany, the lowest-ranked team, rather than a ninth team.

### Outside view

No clean reference class exists. The closest analogue is the IIHF itself. When it implemented the CAS ruling for the 2027 World Juniors, it sent a revised schedule with Russia in Norway's place to federations around 8 Nov, about 9 days after the ruling. Eight days after that, the public WJC schedule page that I could reach (the eu-west.iihf.com mirror) still lists Norway and no Russia. Other federations that lost CAS cases on Russian bans (FIS 2025, luge) complied slowly, through eligibility and neutral-status processes. Status quo over a 10-day window: no change is the default. Prior about 10–15%.

### Inside view

- **For YES.** The CAS award explicitly orders entry to this event. The IIHF has said it is "taking the necessary steps". At the WJC it inserted Russia even though Canada says it will refuse visas. So a host visa ban did not stop it from putting Russia into a schedule. U18W is the next event after the WJC (8 Jan), so it is plausibly next in line. A Germany→Russia swap is mechanically simple.
- **Against.**
  - Nothing found up to 16 Nov reports an IIHF decision on Ostrava, 16 days after the ruling. The WJC was handled within 9 days.
  - The Czech government's visa refusal and the Czech federation's boycott give the IIHF a reason to delay, explore relocation, or treat the event as operationally impossible.
  - Most important: even where the IIHF did act (the WJC), the public page appears not to have changed. Resolution needs the public page, and a relocation might move the field to a new URL only after the window.
- **Combination.** P(IIHF decides a Russian slot at Ostrava by 26 Nov) ≈ 40%, times P(public pages reflect it by the cutoff) ≈ 35%, gives ≈ 14%. A little extra goes to a resolver misreading. Forecast **13%**.

### Evidence

- 30 Oct 2026: CAS ordered the IIHF to admit Russia to five 2027 championships, including U18W Ostrava [1][2].
- 2–3 Nov: Czech PM Babiš said there will be no visas; the interior minister said the 2023 rule still stands; Daily Faceoff wrote that Germany would be removed if Russia plays [3].
- 7 Nov: the IIHF said it is "taking the necessary steps" and is exploring all avenues; Belarus stays out [2].
- ~8 Nov: Norway was informed that Russia replaces it as team #10 at the WJC; Switzerland plans to skip its 27 Dec game against Russia on the revised schedule [4][5].
- 16 Nov: the IIHF WJC schedule (eu-west mirror) still lists NOR and no RUS [6]. iihf.com returned 403 for the WW18 pages [7].
- No reports of relocation, a 9-team format, or a decision specific to U18W (scouts' Czech, Russian and English searches).
