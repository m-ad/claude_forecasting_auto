## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-02

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the CEC's final count of distinct people whose application to be nominated for the 27 January 2027 presidential election it received during the nomination period. Applications close at 24:00 on 12 November 2026, and the nomination stage closes on 13 November. Party nominees and self-nominees both count. Applicants who later withdraw or are refused still count. Documents the CEC refused at intake do not count. The count is of applications received, not registered candidates. The range is 0 to 80, and the upper bound is open. As of 2 November 2026 the CEC has recorded 3 applicants: Kasenov (14 Oct), Esenbekov (27 Oct) and Zhapykeev (28 Oct). Scout checks of the CEC news page found no filing from 29 October to 2 November. With about 10.5 days left, the gap to the 2017 and 2021 totals (~60) is about 57 filings. The incumbent Japarov has said he will run but has not yet filed, so the floor is effectively 4.

### Outside view

Kyrgyz presidential elections draw mass cheap self-nomination. Past totals are 83 applications in 2011 [7], 59 in 2017 [5] and 65 in 2020/21 [3][4], so n = 3 with a mean of about 69. Filing is back-loaded:

| Election | Count before deadline | Final |
|---|---|---|
| 2020/21 | 5 self-nominees on 3 Nov, 11 days out [8]; 24 on the morning of 13 Nov; 34 at 20:30 on 13 Nov (deadline 14 Nov) [3] | 65 [4] |
| 2017 | 41 on 27 Jul, 5 days before the 1 Aug deadline [5]; 46 on 28 Jul [6] | 59 |

Under the current regime the 2025 snap parliamentary election still drew 589 nominees for 90 seats, about 50 of them by 6 October and the rest by 30 October [9]. Mass filing has survived the political tightening. The raw reference value is about 60.

### Inside view

- **Pace.** In 2017, 2 weeks before the deadline, there were probably about 30 applicants. 2026 has 3, an order of magnitude fewer. The 2020 analogue is closer: 5 applicants 11 days out became 65. But the 2020 window had opened only about 9 days earlier, while the 2026 window has been open about 5 weeks. Per day, 2026 is roughly 5× slower than 2020. Scaling today's ~3–4 by the 2020 surge ratio (~13×) gives ~45. Scaling by the 2017 ratio of final total to count 5–11 days out (~1.5–2×), assuming the surge still comes, gives ~8–15.
- **Climate.** This is the first race with a consolidated incumbent who won 79% last time. Heavyweights such as Tashiev, Babanov, Keldibekov and Sariyev have declined. Opposition figures have been jailed or pushed out. The 2020 race was an open post-uprising contest, which likely sits at the top of the range.
- **Language certificate.** Press reports tie the B2/"Выше среднего" certificate to registration, not filing [1][10]. But Zhapykeev filed only after retaking the exam, and the question writer reads that as the test gating filing. If that is true, fringe filings shrink a lot. I give this about 40%.
- I combine these as a mixture: about 55% a "muted surge" (8–20), about 30% a "classic surge" (20–50) and about 15% very low (4–8).

Percentiles: p5 5, p10 7, p25 10, p50 16, p75 26, p90 39, p95 49. The right tail stays wide on purpose, because a classic Kyrgyz last-day surge to 40+ is entirely plausible.

### Evidence

- 3 applicants, all self-nominated: Kasenov (14 Oct), Esenbekov (27 Oct), Zhapykeev (28 Oct) [1][2]. On 30 October the CEC registered authorised representatives for Esenbekov and Zhapykeev, which are not new applications. The CEC site, checked 2 Nov, shows no new applicant [2].
- Japarov announced on 14 Aug 2026 that he will run, and he filed on the last day in 2020 [11]. Suvanaliev has signalled he will run but has not filed. Tashiev will not run [2].
- In 2020 the language test came after filing, and those who failed were removed [10]. 2026 press reports place the certificate at registration [1].
- 2025 parliamentary race: 589 nominees for 90 seats, with filing heavily back-loaded [9].

### What would change my mind

- **Language test before filing.** Confirmation that the language certificate must accompany the application would pull the median toward about 10. Confirmation that it does not, together with any burst of filings in early November, would push it toward 25–30.
- **CEC interim counts.** A CEC interim count of 10 or more by about 9 November would put the classic-surge branch in play.
