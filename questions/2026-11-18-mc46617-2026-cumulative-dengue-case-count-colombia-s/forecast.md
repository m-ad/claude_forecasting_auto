## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-18

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the 2026 year-to-date dengue total (all clinical classifications combined) in the dengue section of the latest INS Boletín Epidemiológico Semanal posted on or before 27 Nov 2026 (UTC−5). That will normally be the EW46 issue (15–21 Nov, expected 26–27 Nov), or the EW45 issue (8–14 Nov, expected ~19–20 Nov) if EW46 is late. Arithmetic: the latest issue found is EW44 (1–7 Nov), cumulative **102,164** (press citing the INS, 17 Nov [1]). EW43 was 97,894 [2], so the EW44 issue added +4,270. The resolving EW46 issue is **two issues** after EW44, or one if EW45 resolves instead. Range 97k–111k, both bounds open. At ~3,900 per issue, EW46 ≈ 110,000, which is near the top of the range.

### Outside view

Per-issue increments in 2026: EW38→EW43 averaged ~2,900 per week; the EW43 issue added 3,821 (1,175 of them late notifications); the EW44 issue added 4,270. In 2025, November issues added far less: EW45 112,955 → EW48 117,710 [4], ~1,600 per issue, and the 2025 EW48 issue added 2,099 [5]. 2026 has run hotter than 2025 since September (outbreak status, El Niño declared 8 Oct).

### Inside view

Base: 102,164 plus 2 × ~3,800 (range 2,800–4,800 per issue) ≈ 109,800. Small discount for a possible plateau toward 2025's seasonal pace (-400). Mixture with a ~15% chance that EW46 is posted late and EW45 (~106,000) resolves (-600 on the median, fatter lower tail). The EW44 number comes only from a secondary source (Diario del Sur; the INS site was unreachable), so I widened the tails slightly. Result: median 109,600, IQR 108,300–110,900. Roughly 30% of the mass is above the 111k open upper bound.
