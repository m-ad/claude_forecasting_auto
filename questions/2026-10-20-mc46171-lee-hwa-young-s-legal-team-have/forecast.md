## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-20

### What resolves YES

The question resolves YES if, by 23:59 KST on 2026-10-31, the Suwon High Court has received a retrial petition (재심청구서) from Lee Hwa-young's legal team that targets his finalized Ssangbangwool North Korea remittance conviction (7 years 8 months, finalized June 2025). A mailed petition counts if the court received it by that date, even if this is reported later. Reporting from Yonhap or other credible outlets decides it. A petition in a different case (e.g. the third-party bribery case), or an announcement without the court receiving anything, resolves NO. As of 2026-10-16 the court had received nothing.

### Outside view

I found no clean reference class. The closest is a defence team that has publicly committed to a cheap, unilateral procedural filing with a stated deadline ("this month") 2–3 weeks away. Such filings usually happen, and roughly on time, because only the team has to act. The common failure is slippage: drafting takes longer, or the team waits for evidence (the NIS documents) or another proceeding (the prosecutor Park Sang-yong probe). This is my judgement, not a counted base rate. I put roughly 70–80% on such a filing landing within the stated month, and less when the window is short and drafting was unfinished at announcement.

### Inside view

- **For YES:** On 10-13 the five lawyers formally decided to file at the Suwon High Court "within October". They dropped parole and every other option, so the retrial is now their only route. On 10-14 they named "next week" (10-19 to 10-23). The client has been held since 2022, is pressing for action, and was earlier quoted as angry at the slow pace. On 10-05 the team said it "cannot delay the retrial process". Filing needs no one's permission, and mail is acceptable. Eleven days remain, including all of the week they announced.
- **For NO:** As of 10-14 the petition still had to be drafted after more meetings with Lee in detention. Lawyer Ryu said no date was fixed and it could change. The core evidence is a level-2 classified NIS document, which may complicate how it is attached or cited. A strategy shift is possible, for example waiting for the Park Sang-yong disciplinary or investigation outcome, or political sensitivity because the president is a co-defendant. My searches found no report since 10-14, so no filing on 10-19/10-20 is visible yet. Thin indexing means that is weak evidence.
- There is also a small resolver risk: a mailed filing could go unreported, or be logged after 10-31.

Net: I start at about 75% and shade down slightly for the unfinished draft and the explicit "could change". The central estimate is 72%.
