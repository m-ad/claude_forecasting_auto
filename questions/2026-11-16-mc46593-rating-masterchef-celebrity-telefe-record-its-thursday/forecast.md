### Inside view

I start from the recent-Thursday level of about 12.7. I take off about 0.2 for late-November softening (warmer weather, end of the school year) and mid-season contestant attrition, which gives a median of 12.4. The spread uses the night-to-night SD of about 0.6, widened for a 10-day horizon, for possible schedule shifts (a later start loses audience), and for an occasional special gala that lifts the number (the season peak on this basis is 13.4). I see no known scheduled disruption on Nov 26: the Libertadores final is Saturday Nov 28, and the reported Betular absence starts only in late December.
