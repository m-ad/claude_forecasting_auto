## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-18

### What resolves YES

YES if, by 28 Nov 2026 23:59 Paraguay time (UTC−3), the BCP (including its Equipo Interventor) or the FGD has publicly stated a specific calendar day on which FGD payments to ueno bank CDA holders begin or began. The day may be before or after 28 Nov, the statement may cover only some CDA holders, and the first qualifying statement settles it. Vague periods ("next stage", "in December"), registration/validation deadlines and payments to other claimant classes do not count. The arithmetic: there are 10 days left in the window (18–28 Nov). The legal deadline to honour the guarantee is 15 calendar days from the resolution, i.e. 17 Nov, extendable to 2 Dec [4]. That deadline falls inside or right after the window.

### Outside view

For "will an authority name a date by D" questions the status-quo prior is usually low. Here, though, an FGD payout is legally mandated with a near deadline, and the BCP has issued payment communiqués almost daily. The base rate for "a central bank in an active payout gives a dated schedule for the next tranche within ~2 weeks" is high, but I have no clean n.

### Inside view

The decisive fact is that this has probably **already happened**. On 13 Nov 2026 (07:19), La Nación reported that the BCP announced CDA payments "arrancarán este viernes" (start this Friday, i.e. 13 Nov), with funds "se irán liberando por cortes a lo largo del día" (released in batches during the day) as verifications completed [1]. On 16 Nov the FGD director said 5,800 CDA-linked cases had been unblocked by Friday [2]. Under the criteria, a stated day "before" the deadline counts and the first statement settles the question. That is a direct BCP statement giving a calendar day, reported by a credible outlet. Risks to YES:
- The question's own background (written later) says no CDA date had been announced as of 13 Nov. An LLM resolver anchored on that might go looking for a *new* statement.
- The resolver might classify the 13 Nov release as the 40-wage anticipo applied to CDA holders, which the fine print excludes as "deposits already covered by the anticipo". That reading is strained, because those payments are FGD payments to CDA holders.

A separate path to YES is a dated announcement for the 75-wage complement, which includes CDA holders, before 28 Nov. The 17 Nov legal deadline and its 2 Dec extension make that likely too. Net: about 85%.

### Evidence

- 8 Nov 2026: BCP said CDAs are not in the first two payment groups and that later stages would be announced [3].
- 12 Nov 2026: BCP opened a CDA ownership-validation platform (question background).
- 13 Nov 2026: BCP announced the release of CDA funds "desde este viernes" (from this Friday, 13 Nov) [1].
- 16 Nov 2026: FGD director José Maciel said 5,800 CDA cases were unblocked by Friday and remaining blocked funds would be available Monday [2].
- The legal deadline for the FGD guarantee is 17 Nov, extendable to 2 Dec [4].

### What would change my mind

- Evidence that the 13 Nov release covered only unblocking of sight-deposit anticipos for people who also hold CDAs, rather than CDA balances themselves. That would cut the forecast to roughly 50%, which is roughly the base rate for a dated complement announcement within 10 days.
- An official BCP communiqué saying CDA payments remain unscheduled.
