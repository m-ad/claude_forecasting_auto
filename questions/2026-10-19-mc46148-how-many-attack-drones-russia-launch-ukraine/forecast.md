## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-19

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to one number: how many attack and decoy drones (Shahed-type, Gerbera, Parodiya and similar) the Ukrainian Air Force says Russia launched or detected during the overnight attack that starts on the evening of 29 Oct 2026 (Kyiv time). The figure comes from the Air Force's morning report of 30 Oct, as relayed by Ukrinform or other credible outlets. It counts drones launched, not drones shot down, and excludes missiles. It covers only that one night: daytime updates and multi-day totals don't count. If the Air Force publishes no figure for the night, the question is annulled. Both bounds are open, so values below 40 or above 700 are possible.

### Outside view

**Reference class:** the Air Force's nightly launch counts for the 30 days before the question was written. The question text lists normal nights for early to mid October 2026: 135, 137, 130, 142, 136, 128 and 149. Their median is about 136, and they cluster between 128 and 149. Large combined strikes come roughly every 1–2 weeks. Those include about 580 drones on the night of 15 Oct and more than 450 on 13 Sep. Quieter nights also occur. One such night in mid-October had about 70 drones (Ukrainska Pravda, via Yahoo, year not confirmed). In 2025–26, roughly 10–15% of nights carried a massive strike of 400 or more drones.

**Base-rate shape:** a lognormal centred near 140, plus a mixture component for a massive strike with about 10–12% weight.

### Inside view

- **Upward adjustments:**
  - Russia's winter campaign against energy infrastructure began in late September.
  - The question says barrages have grown larger through 2026.
  - On the night of 18–19 Oct, air defences shot down 243 drones (Ukrinform). That implies roughly 260–290 launched, which is above the "normal night" cluster.
- **Combined effect:** these move the median from about 136 up to about 160 and fatten the 200–300 range.
- **Uncertainty:** the night of 29–30 Oct (Thursday into Friday) is 10 days out, so I can't know whether it will be a massive-strike night. I put about 12% on a massive night of 400 or more drones and about 4% on more than 600. Below 80 drones gets about 5%.

### Evidence

- Early to mid October 2026, from the question text: normal nights had 128–149 drones. On 15 Oct a combined strike used 580 drones, and there were more than 850 over 14–15 Oct. On 13 Sep, more than 450 drones were launched. In September, more than 3,177 jet drones were used over the month. [1]
- 19 Oct 2026 at 09:50: air defences shot down 243 drones and 4 missiles overnight, with hits recorded at 25 locations. The headline gives no launch total. [2]
- An undated Ukrinform report says 135 drones were detected overnight from 17 Oct, with 80 shot down. The year is not confirmed. [3]
- Ukrainska Pravda, via Yahoo, reports 70 drones launched from the evening of 16 Oct, with 35 downed. The year is not confirmed, but this shows that low nights happen. [4]
- Historical reference: 479 drones in June 2025, then a war record. Massive strikes have recurred since. [5]

### What would change my mind

- **Massive-strike timing:** whether Russia stages a massive combined strike on 29–30 Oct specifically. A strike on that night would send the outcome to 400–700. Tight timing, such as a large strike only a day or two earlier, would make a smaller night more likely.
- **Ceasefire or negotiation news:** any ceasefire or negotiation-driven pause would shift mass sharply downward.
