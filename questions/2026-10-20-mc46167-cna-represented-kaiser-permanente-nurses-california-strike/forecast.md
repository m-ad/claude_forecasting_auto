## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-20

### What resolves YES

The question resolves YES if, at any time on 29 or 30 October 2026 (Pacific Time), CNA/NNU-represented registered nurses at Kaiser Permanente facilities in California are on an organised, union-sanctioned work stoppage against Kaiser. That covers a one-day, multi-day, open-ended or sympathy strike, confirmed by CNA/NNU or by credible reporting. Pickets and rallies where nurses keep working do not count. Strikes by other unions (UNAC/UHCP, NUHW) count only if CNA nurses also withhold labor. Section 8(g) of the National Labor Relations Act (NLRA) requires 10 days' written notice before any strike at a health-care institution, and CNA says it never gives less [2][8]. A strike on 29 October therefore needs a notice served by 19 October, and one on 30 October needs a notice by 20 October.

### Outside view

- **CNA–Kaiser contract cycles since 2011:** CNA struck for one day in September 2011 and again in January 2012, and for two days in November 2014. In 2015 and 2022 a tentative agreement (TA) came 1 to 4 days before a noticed strike, so no strike took place. In 2017–18 no notice was ever served [5][6][7].
- **Any given 2-day window:** even in strike-prone cycles, CNA strikes at Kaiser last 1 to 2 days, so the share of days spent on strike is tiny. Without a notice already served, the base rate for a specific 2-day window about 9 days away is about 1% or less.
- **The binding constraint:** a strike in the window needs a notice served by 19–20 October. The real question is whether one was served on 16–20 October without being reported yet.

### Inside view

- **No notice as of 15 October.** On 15 October Kaiser wrote that CNA had held a strike authorization vote but that "We have not received such a notice" [2].
- **CNA's own press page is silent.** Its newest release is dated 17 October. It has published no vote result, no notice and no strike date for Kaiser [1]. CNA normally announces a notice with a press release on the day, as in 2022 [6].
- **Bargaining is still active.** The Northern California table meets on 21–22 October and the Los Angeles Medical Center (LAMC) table on 23 October, with dates booked into December [2][3]. A union serving a notice on 19–20 October would be striking right after those sessions. CNA's pattern is to notice after a failed session, which points to an early-November strike at the earliest.
- **No sympathy-strike vehicle.** Searches found no NUHW or other Kaiser union action planned for late October, and UNAC/UHCP has a contract through 2029 [7][9].
- **Upside risks.** 2026 has been an unusually heated year at Kaiser: a 5-week UNAC strike, a CNA sympathy strike in March, and a July lawsuit by Kaiser against CNA [7]. The authorization vote has happened. A notice served on Monday 19 or Tuesday 20 October could still be unindexed. I put that chance at about 3–5%, and a strike that then goes ahead (no TA, no postponement) at about 60%.

Net: about 3%. The deadline maths and a silent union press page bound this from above. I don't go lower because of a possible notice on 19–20 October that I cannot see yet.

### Evidence

- 15 Oct 2026: Kaiser's LAMC update says CNA held a strike authorization vote, "The union is required by law to provide Kaiser Permanente with a 10-day advance notice… We have not received such a notice." The next session is 23 October, with 4 more dates into early December [2].
- 12 Oct 2026: Kaiser's Northern California update counts 17 sessions since July, with the next on 21–22 October and more being confirmed through December. Kaiser is "disappointed that CNA has called for a strike authorization vote" [3].
- 5–17 Oct 2026: the NNU press page has no Kaiser strike, vote result, notice or TA (checked 20 Oct) [1].
- 1 Sep 2026: informational pickets at 22 Kaiser facilities, with no work stoppage [9].
- 2022 precedent: notice served 10 November for a 21–22 November strike, and a TA on 17 November averted it [6].
