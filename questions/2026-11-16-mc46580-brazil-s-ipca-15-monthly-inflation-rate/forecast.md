## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the month-on-month change in IBGE's IPCA-15 for the November 2026 reference period, at two decimals (e.g. 0.32). IBGE publishes it on 26 Nov 2026 at 09:00 Brasília time. Collection runs from about 16 Oct to 15 Nov 2026, so the window is half October and half November. The last published IPCA-15 is October (+0.32%, collected 16 Sep–15 Oct, released 23 Oct). November is the next edition, one period ahead. The range is open at both ends (-0.5 to 0.9), so tails outside it are allowed. Only the monthly rate counts, not the 12-month figure.

### Outside view

Recent November IPCA-15 prints: 0.33% (2023), 0.62% (2024), 0.20% (2025). The 2026 monthly path has been volatile: +0.41, +0.06, -0.40, +0.70, +0.32 (June–October), mostly because of the electricity tariff flags. Market misses on the first print of the month are typically ±0.1 pp, sometimes more. Before any 2026 evidence, the anchor is about 0.28%, the October print minus a little seasonality, with a standard deviation of about 0.13 pp.

### Inside view

Rough components of the 16 Oct–15 Nov window:
- **Food** (weight ~21%): still hot. The IPC-S food group was +1.07% and IPC-Fipe food +1.36% in early November, and tomatoes are up again. At about 0.9%, food adds roughly +0.19 pp, about the same as or a bit more than October's +0.88%.
- **Electricity** (~4%): the switch to the green flag is still feeding through. IPC-S shows residential electricity at -1.82% in the month to early November, and the green flag holds for November. At about -1%, it takes roughly -0.04 pp, similar to October.
- **Airfares**: they swing from -9.24% (-0.08 pp) in the October IPCA-15 to rises. Jet fuel went up 7.2% on 1 Nov, and IPC-S airfares are +6%. Net swing: about +0.05–0.10 pp versus October.
- **Counterweight**: the full October IPCA was only 0.14%, below every estimate. Excluding electricity it was about 0.2%, and airfares fell. Half of this window is late October, which that soft reading covers.
- **Other forecasts**: 4intelligence has flagged about +0.3%. Fecomércio-RS projects +0.38% for the full November IPCA.

Combining these gives a median of 0.31%. The fat tails reflect a ±0.1 pp miss being routine and possible surprises in electricity or airfares.

### What would change my mind

- **2nd-quadrissemana IPC-S/IPC-Fipe readings** (due 16–17 Nov). A drop toward 0.2% would point to about 0.25%. A rise above 0.45% would point to about 0.4%.
- **Electricity in the IPCA-15**: if IBGE's pro-rata treatment puts most of the flag change into October, the drag is smaller, which adds about +0.03 pp.
