## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-02

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the cumulative number of patients the Mendoza Ministry of Health and Sports reports as laboratory-confirmed (bacterial isolation) *S. marcescens* infections linked to the Hospital Privado, in its most recent official statement made on or before 13 Nov 2026 (UTC−3). Suspected, probable, under-study and discarded patients are not counted; the deaths-under-study are not counted until confirmed. If the ministry says nothing new, the last official figure holds. Arithmetic: latest figure is 5 (Oct 27, repeated Oct 29–30), up from 4 on Oct 24. Pending pool: 3 suspected (two of them the suspected deaths without microbiological confirmation), possibly two press-reported "possible cases" (La Nación Oct 28: "would be 7 if confirmed"), and 3 now reported negative (Oct 30). To reach 8 needs at least 3 further confirmations, i.e. nearly the whole pending pool plus a new case. Confirmed set: 3 meningitis (CSF), 1 blood isolate, 1 surgical-site infection after a September operation.

### Outside view

No exact reference class. Hospital point-source Serratia clusters (contaminated anaesthetic/infusion/antiseptic) usually grow modestly after the first tally while case-finding continues, then plateau once procedures stop (Greece cardiac-surgery cluster 23/47 patients; spinal-anaesthesia cluster 12/46; Sarajevo orthopaedic cluster 79 patients; sizes very variable, n small and anecdotal). Here surgery in the implicated rooms stopped about Oct 20–22 and the count has been flat for ~6 days (Oct 27 – Oct 30 at least). Status quo (the last official number stands) is the strongest prior: 5.

### Inside view

Starting point 5. Upside: pending suspected cases (3, plus two possible), unreported lab results, retrospective case-finding among September–October surgical patients, Malbrán genotyping or OR cultures revealing more. Downside: the ministry may reclassify (the September SSI patient may be considered separate or dropped, a small probability), and there is no dashboard so updates may be sparse. The ministry also has little incentive to publish further counts if nothing changes. Suspected deaths probably lack cultures, so they may remain "suspected". My distribution: 4 ≈ 3 %, 5 ≈ 58 %, 6 ≈ 17 %, 7 ≈ 10 %, 8 ≈ 5 %, 9+ ≈ 7 %. The one adjustment from the last value is the right-skew (added probability of 6+), which rests on the pending pool; the flat count since Oct 27 and the three negatives keep the median at 5.
