## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

This is a discrete question with outcomes 0, 1 or 2. It counts how many of ChAdOx1 BDBV (Oxford/Serum Institute of India) and mRNA-1469 (Moderna) have at least one dose given to a participant of the Tumaini ring-vaccination trial (NCT07842107, DRC). The dose must be reported by a source published by **27 Nov 2026, 23:59 UTC**, and the source must explicitly tie it to Tumaini. Preferred sources are WHO, the trial sponsors and developers, and the DRC Ministry of Health; the ebolaintel tracker and other credible outlets are secondary. Ervebo doses do not count, and neither do doses given in other trials (Phase 1 trials in the UK, Uganda or Canada, PARTNERS, EBO-PEP). Timeline: today is 16 Nov, so 11 days remain. WHO recommended the candidates on 4 Nov, which leaves 23 days from the recommendation to the cutoff. As of 12–16 Nov no Tumaini dose of either candidate had been reported, so the count today is 0.

### Outside view

There is no clean reference class. Experimental outbreak vaccines with doses and protocol already in place reached first dose fast: Uganda Sudan virus 2025 in 4 days, Rwanda Marburg 2024 in about 10 days. Where doses still had to be shipped and approvals obtained, it took far longer: Uganda 2022 doses arrived 79 days after the outbreak was declared, and no one was ever dosed. Here the trial platform is already running, which is the fast-case feature. National approval, the protocol amendment through ACOREP (the DRC's national ethics committee) and shipment are still outstanding, which is the slow-case feature. WHO's own guidance on 4 Nov was a start "within three to four weeks", which means 25 Nov to 2 Dec, after national approval. Official start-date guidance usually slips. My prior is about 15% that at least one candidate is dosed and publicly reported within 11 days.

### Inside view

- **Pushes toward a fast start:** this is the largest Bundibugyo outbreak on record (more than 11,000 confirmed cases, Rt about 1.25, and Africa CDC said on 15 Nov it is "not under control"). Tumaini is an adaptive platform built to add arms. The Serum Institute holds about 620,000 ChAdOx1 BDBV doses, and Phase 1 safety data exist. A first dose could be announced quickly, since CEPI and Oxford press-release first doses.
- **Pushes toward a slow start:** there was no report of DRC approval, shipment or a start date in the Gavi piece (10 Nov), the MSF statement (10 Nov) or the tracker (12 Nov). MSF said on 10 Nov that Bundibugyo-specific vaccines are "unlikely to be available in the near term". Only one Tumaini site was open on 11 Nov. Even WHO's optimistic 3–4 week window mostly ends after 27 Nov. A dose given on 26–27 Nov might also not be reported before the cutoff.
- **Each candidate separately:** ChAdOx1 is more likely than mRNA-1469. Its bulk doses already exist and it ships on an ordinary cold chain. mRNA-1469 has only about 12,000 vials, still unfilled, and needs a frozen cold chain. The two candidates may share one approval and amendment, which correlates their outcomes. Getting both counted needs both products on site.
- **Final distribution:** P(0) = 0.78, P(1) = 0.17, P(2) = 0.05.

### Evidence

- On 4 Nov 2026, WHO expert groups recommended adding both candidates. They would enter only "following national approvals" [3][6].
- WHO Science Director Meg Doherty said on 4 Nov that trials could start "within three to four weeks" once DRC regulators approve, and noted the approval had not been issued [3].
- Gavi, 10 Nov: both candidates "will enter the trial following national approvals". It reported no shipments [4].
- The ebolaintel tracker (updated 12 Nov) reports no Tumaini doses, no approvals and no start date for either candidate [1].
- MSF, 10 Nov: Bundibugyo-specific vaccines are "unlikely to be available in the near term" [5].
- Tumaini began on 23 Oct in Bunia using Ervebo only [2]. The Serum Institute stockpiled about 620,000 ChAdOx1 BDBV doses, and no shipment to the DRC has been reported [7].
- Base-rate cases: Uganda 2025 reached first dose 4 days after declaration with pre-positioned doses; Rwanda 2024 about 10 days; Uganda 2022 had doses arrive on day 79 and dosed no one [8][9].
