## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the year-on-year % change, at one decimal, of 生鮮食品を除く総合 (total index excluding fresh food) for the Tokyo Ku-area. The survey month is November 2026, and the figure is taken from Table 1 of the Statistics Bureau's mid-month preliminary release. That release is scheduled for 2026-11-27 08:30 JST (2026-11-26 23:30 UTC). The figure as first published governs.

**Periods:** the last published figure is October 2026 (released 2026-10-30): +2.6% y/y, index 103.3. The target is the next month, a single step ahead.

**Arithmetic:** Nov y/y ≈ 2.6 + (Nov 2026 m/m − Nov 2025 m/m). In 2025 the rate was flat from October to November (2.8 → 2.8), so the base month had no unusual move.

**Bins:** the outcome space has 15 bins from 1.8 to 3.2, with open tails beyond each end.

### Outside view

Recent month-to-month changes in this series run 0.0–0.3 pp (Sep→Oct −0.1; Aug 1.8 → Sep 2.7 was a water-charge special factor). A no-change prior centred on 2.6, with a SD (standard deviation) of about 0.15–0.2 pp, is the reference distribution.

### Inside view

- **Energy (the main driver, +0.15 pp).** Japan's summer electricity/gas subsidy covered July–September 2026 usage. It was 3.5 yen/kWh and 14 yen/m³ for September usage, and zero from October usage [3][4]. October usage is billed in November and enters the November CPI. In 2025 the same exit step was smaller: 2.0 yen/kWh and 8 yen/m³ for September 2025 usage (amounts from memory, flagged).
  - The net effect is about +1.5 yen/kWh, roughly +4 pp on electricity y/y. Electricity weighs about 3% of the index, giving about +0.12 pp.
  - Gas adds about +0.05 pp, and more with the Tokyo Gas base-charge rise effective 2026-10-01 [4].
  - The gasoline subsidy expansion of mid-November 2025 mostly hit December 2025, so it adds little here [6].
- **Food (roughly neutral).** Rice deflation is deepening (−31.3% y/y in October [1]), a drag of a few hundredths. Against that, November 2026 has about 10× November 2025's food price-hike items (1,477 vs 143) [5]. Food ex fresh is slowing (3.6 → 3.0).
- **Underlying momentum.** Ex-fresh-food-and-energy slowed from 2.9 to 2.6, but the seasonally adjusted m/m was +0.3.
- **Result.** A median of about 2.77, so 2.7 and 2.8 are close to equally likely, with modest mass on 2.6 and 2.9 or higher. The tails are kept wide because the subsidy sizes and weights rest partly on search snippets.

### Evidence

- 2026-10-30, the October release: core +2.6% (consensus 2.6), all items +2.8, core-core +2.6. Energy rose from −1.9% to +2.2% (electricity +1.7, gas +4.9, gasoline −2.3), and rice fell 31.3% [1][2].
- Nov 2025 base: Tokyo core 2.8% y/y, unchanged from October 2025. Electricity and gas widened their contribution then too [7].
- 2026 subsidy: September usage 3.5 yen/kWh and 14 yen/m³, October usage 0 [3][4].
- Teikoku Databank: 1,477 food items rise in price in November 2026, against 143 in November 2025 [5].

### What would change my mind

- Evidence that the 2025 summer subsidy exit was as large as 2026's would remove the energy uplift, putting the median back at 2.6.
- A sharp fall in hotel or package-tour prices, or a faster rice drop, would pull the figure toward 2.5.
