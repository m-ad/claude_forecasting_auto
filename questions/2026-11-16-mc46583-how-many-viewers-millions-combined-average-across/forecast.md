## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the combined average audience ('audiencia media'), in millions, of a leaders' debate aired on Spanish linear TV on 26 Nov 2026. The figure is summed across every channel that carried it in full, as published by Barlovento Comunicación (Kantar). If Barlovento gives no combined number, the per-channel figures are added. To qualify, the debate needs ≥ 2 of the cabezas de lista of PSOE, PP, Vox and Frente Amplio; spokesperson panels such as laSexta's 26 Nov "Mesa Abierta" do not count. If no qualifying debate airs, the question is **annulled**, so this forecast is conditional on one taking place. As of 10–13 Nov, the 26 Nov four-way (RTVE's original proposal, pushed by the PP, to be produced by the Academia de Televisión) had **no confirmed date, line-up or carriers** [1][2]. Arithmetic on the bounds: the range is 2–10M and both ends are open. The last comparable values are 5.911M (2023 cara a cara, two channels) and 4.155M (2023 RTVE three-way). The 2019 Academia five-way drew 8.62M, and La 1 alone contributed 3.40M of that [3][4][5].

### Outside view

Reference class: Spanish national election debates, 2015–2023 (n = 8, Barlovento/Kantar). They drew 8.6–10.5M in 2015–2019, then fell to 5.9M (cara a cara) and 4.16M (three-way without Feijóo) in July 2023. Each 2023 debate was the least watched of its type. The relevant prior is therefore the 2023 level: roughly 4–6M, centred near 5M, with ±25 % spread.

### Inside view

- **Linear TV has stopped declining.** Daily linear reach fell from 28.2M (2023) to 26.7M (2025), but October 2026 viewing was 159 min/day, +1 % year on year [6]. November is also a heavier viewing season than July, when the 2023 debates aired. Both effects are small and positive.
- **Format and carriage are the swing factor.** An Academia institutional signal carried by La 1, Antena 3, laSexta and the regional channels, with all four leaders, three days before the vote, in a high-salience campaign (the Urda crash, the 11 Nov strike), could reach 6–7M. That would be close to the 2023 cara a cara and well below 2019. A single-group broadcast, or one with an empty chair, would look more like the 2023 three-way: 3–4M.
- **Dilution.** The cara a cara on 19 Nov and the Atresmedia four-way on 23 Nov come first. In 2019, a second four-way on consecutive days still held 9.5M, so fatigue is modest. Still, the 26 Nov debate would be the third leaders' event in a week.
- **Current anchors.** A Nations League match drew 4.14M on 12 Nov. The laSexta spokesperson debate drew 1.33M on 13 Nov [7]. Prime-time audiences are weak.

Net: the median is 5.0M, with a wide, right-skewed distribution.

### Evidence

- 2026-11-10: Demócrata's calendar lists the 26 Nov four-way only as RTVE's "propuesta previa". The Academia's debate among the main candidates had no date yet and its format was pending [1].
- 2026-11-13: the Academia's note confirms only the 19 Nov cara a cara, produced as an institutional signal for any channel [2].
- The PP says there will be no four-way on RTVE; Feijóo has said he will attend a four-way, with 26 Nov floated [8].
- 2023: the cara a cara drew 5.911M / 46.5 % on Antena 3 + laSexta. The RTVE three-way drew 4.155M / 34.6 %, of which La 1 accounted for 3.46M [3][5].
- 4 Nov 2019: the Academia five-way drew 8.62M / 52.7 % across about 11 channels [4].
- 2026-11-13: the laSexta spokesperson debate drew 1.328M / 11.9 % [7].

### What would change my mind

- The 19 Nov cara a cara figure (published 20 Nov). Below 4.5M would put the 26 Nov debate's median near 4M; above 6.5M would put it near 6M.
- Carriage and line-up. Simulcast on La 1 plus Atresmedia with all four leaders would move the median up to about 5.8M. A single network, or Sánchez or Feijóo absent, would move it down to about 3.8M.
