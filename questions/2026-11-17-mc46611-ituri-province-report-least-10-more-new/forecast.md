### Inside view

The trend runs against Ituri keeping its lead. WHO (15–16 Nov) says new cases in Bunia have **halved**, and that Nizi, Rwampara, Mongbwalu and Nyankunde are under control. Ituri provincial authorities (16 Nov) report declines in several zones. In Nord-Kivu, Africa CDC reports resurgence over the last 21 days in Beni, Butembo and Oicha. The government already said on 6 Nov that Nord-Kivu notifies the most new cases. Against that, Ituri out-reported Nord-Kivu on most of the last 11 days, Ituri also has its own resurgent zones (Fataki, Kilo, Drodro, Mahagi), and Katwa in Nord-Kivu is declining. Nine days ahead, with a flat national total of about 60–65 a day, I expect the mean gap to drift down to roughly +5 to +7. With a standard deviation of about 14, that gives P(gap ≥ 10) ≈ 40 %. Reporting batches (lab backlogs) add variance on both sides.
