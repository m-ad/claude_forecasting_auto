## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-20

### What resolves YES

This resolves YES only if, before 1 November 2026, a **named** US, NATO or Ukrainian government official says on the record (as reported by credible media), or an official US/NATO/Ukrainian government statement confirms, that the US has **suspended, halted or stopped** delivering US-made weapons procured under PURL. A halt of only part of PURL (e.g. Patriot interceptors) counts. These do not count: "delayed", "slowed" or "at risk"; threats; a halt limited to intelligence sharing or Starshield; a refusal to approve *new* packages without a halt to funded ones; and general Trump remarks about keeping ammunition, unless they refer to PURL deliveries being stopped. Anonymous officials do not satisfy the "named official" route. The question was written on 20 October, after Zelensky's 18 October remarks, and describes the status as "contested". I therefore read those remarks as not, by themselves, resolving YES.

### Outside view

There is no clean reference class. The closest is earlier US pauses of arms flows to Ukraine under Trump: March 2025 (aid and intelligence) and July 2025 (Pentagon stockpile pause on Patriots and other munitions). Both were confirmed by officials within 1–2 days of the first leak and reversed within about 1–2 weeks. Threats of that kind are far more frequent than pauses that actually happen. Trump threatened PURL over Hormuz in April 2026 and over the refinery strikes in October 2026, and neither threat was carried out. By my rough count, perhaps 1 in 4 to 1 in 3 serious US threats against Ukraine aid became an acknowledged pause within a month. The window is only 11 days, so the outside view is roughly 10–15%.

### Inside view

Several factors push the number up from the base rate:
- **There is a partial hold now.** Kyiv Independent and the Washington Post (19 Oct) report that Hegseth has not signed off on a large batch of Patriot interceptors that allies have already paid for under PURL [3][4]. Zelensky said the US "approved the delivery of only some of them, but not the rest. And this is not a technical issue" [3]. That is one step from the qualifying wording. If Zelensky, Defence Minister Fedorov or Foreign Minister Sybiha says plainly that the US has "stopped" or "blocked" PURL Patriots, it resolves YES. Zelensky speaks publicly almost every day and is openly angry ("betrayed").
- **The escalation is live.** Ukraine resumed refinery strikes on 15 Oct ("The block is out. We're done") [6]. Trump called them a "disgrace". The US midterms on 3 Nov make diesel prices politically salient. The White House has already shown it will degrade support quietly (Starshield limits, Reuters 16 Oct) [7].

Other factors pull it down:
- **Officials keep saying deliveries continue.** Every on-record US and NATO statement so far says so: Whitaker on 13 Oct ("no change", "short-term challenge"), NATO officials on 12 and 16 Oct, and Rutte [1][2][5]. The US denies the threats.
- **"Delay" is the convenient framing.** It suits every party. Washington wants leverage without the July-2025-style backlash; NATO wants PURL to look reliable; even Kyiv may avoid declaring PURL dead. A withheld signature can stay a "delay" indefinitely, and only "delayed" language resolves NO.
- **The window is short and the criteria strict.** A named official and halt wording are both required, and there are 11 days.

Taken together, I land at about 20%, which is above the base rate because of the partial Patriot hold and the risk that the resolver accepts Zelensky-style wording. The IQR runs from 13% to 30%. Most of the uncertainty is about how the LLM resolver will read Ukrainian statements of the kind "approved only part".

### Evidence

- 2026-10-09/11: Miami talks; US envoys pressed Kyiv over refinery strikes. FT: the US threatened to halt PURL and intelligence sharing. The White House declined to comment [1][6].
- 2026-10-12: An anonymous NATO official: "Support for Ukraine via PURL continues to arrive" [2].
- 2026-10-13: US NATO Ambassador Whitaker: "I know of no change to that programme." He admitted air-defence delays and called them a "short-term challenge" [5].
- 2026-10-15: Ukraine struck a Ryazan refinery, ending its pause. Trump on Newsmax: "We've been keeping the ammunition for ourselves lately" (no PURL reference) [6].
- 2026-10-15/16: WaPo: Ukraine is almost out of Patriots. A senior NATO official: Patriot interceptors "continue to flow". Reuters: the White House ordered Starshield limits [4][7].
- 2026-10-18/19: Zelensky to Bloomberg: the US approved only some PURL-funded Patriots. Kyiv Independent: Hegseth "isn't signing them". The US has given no public explanation [3].

### What would change my mind

- A named Ukrainian official saying the US has "stopped" or "blocked" PURL Patriot deliveries would make this near-certain YES. A Hegseth signature or a reported Patriot arrival would push it down sharply.
- A formal US decision to cut PURL or intelligence after further refinery strikes, which a leak plus official comment would likely confirm within a day, as in March and July 2025.
