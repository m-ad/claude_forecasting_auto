## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-02

### What resolves YES

The BBWAA announces the 2026 AL MVP on 2026-11-12 (question resolves 2026-11-13). YES only if the winner is listed first on every ballot in the official tally: 30 ballots, two writers in each of 15 AL cities. If fewer than 30 ballots are counted, the winner must be first on every ballot counted. One first-place vote for anyone else (a second Witt, Caminero, Schlittler or other) makes it NO. No arithmetic on reporting periods applies. The threshold is 30/30 first-place votes. In the last expert poll (MLB.com, 2026-09-22) Alvarez had 29 of 38 first-place votes (76%).

### Outside view

Reference class: AL MVP winners, 2000–2025 (n=26). Unanimous in 2014, 2021, 2023 and 2024 only, about 15%. Among clear runaway winners the rate is higher, roughly 40%. Judge 2022 (62 HR) got 28/30, while Ohtani 2021/2023, Trout 2014 and Judge 2024 got 30/30. All 12 AL unanimous winners through 2023 were runaways. Voters usually consolidate behind the favourite more than expert polls suggest: in 2022 the MLB.com poll showed Judge with 72% of first-place votes and the BBWAA gave him 93%.

### Inside view

P(Alvarez wins) is about 95–97% (Kalshi ~90% in August, ~97% on an aggregator on Oct 27). Conditional on winning, P(30/30) is about 18–20%.

- **Against unanimity:** The last MLB.com poll gave Alvarez 76% of first-place votes, with Caminero at 7 and Witt at 1. Alvarez is a primary DH (some voters discount that). He cooled after Aug 1 (.860 OPS, 4 HR). Witt tied him on fWAR (6.8). Cy Young favourite Schlittler had 7.0 WAR by mid-September, so a pitcher could pick up a first-place vote. Caminero tied for second in HR with 42. There are 30 voters, and regional pairs (e.g. the two writers from one city) correlate dissent.
- **For unanimity:** He led the majors in AVG, OBP, SLG and OPS, led the AL in oWAR, and had a batting-title and near-Triple-Crown profile. There is no rival with a clearly better case. The last expert poll said "clear frontrunners".
- Rough estimate: assume per-voter dissent of about 5% with some clustering. That gives P(0 dissent in 30) of about 20%. Times 0.96 for winning, rounded down for the DH and slump penalty. Result: about 17%.
