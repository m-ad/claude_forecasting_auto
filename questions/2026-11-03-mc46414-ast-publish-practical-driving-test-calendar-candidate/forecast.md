## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-03

### What resolves YES
YES if, by end of 12 Nov 2026 (Europe/Rome), astsicilia.it ("News" or "Lavora con Noi") carries an item stating at least one date on which practical driving tests for the 73-operator selection will be held (partial calendars by venue/batch count; revisions count). Items without dates (requirements-check outcome, admitted/excluded lists) do not count; a revocation/suspension before then without dates is NO. Timeline arithmetic: pre-selection 21–23 Oct; scores notice 26 Oct (last item as of 30 Oct, per the question text); the notice says the calendar follows the art. 8 requirements check. Window from the scores notice to the deadline = 17 days; from the question's last-checked date (30 Oct) to the deadline = 13 days. Secondary sources say the bando bis requires the calendar to be published at least 15 days before the tests begin (the question text says no minimum notice; unverified) — that sets a floor between publication and tests, not a deadline for publication.

### Outside view
Reference class: Italian public-company driver selections, gap between pre-selection scores and the practical-test calendar. I found no hard n; AMAT Palermo (806 candidates) spread tests over 31 Aug–4 Dec with litigation history, and Sicilian operators typically post calendars 1–2 weeks ahead. Status-quo rule: nothing published in the 8 days after the scores (to 3 Nov, unverified by me as the site was unreachable), and the requirement check on hundreds of candidates takes weeks in typical Italian practice. Prior roughly 30–40% for publication within the next ~9 days.

### Inside view
For speed: AST turned scores around in days, the pre-selection calendar was posted 1–2 weeks ahead, and there is service pressure to hire. Against: the art. 8 check, commissions and dual-control buses across six cities, the 15-day notice norm (which would push tests to late Nov/Dec and permit a mid/late-Nov calendar), and a politically fraught history (first bando revoked). No evidence of an announced start date. Net: ~33%. Adjustment recorded once: requirements check/logistics, −20 pp from a speed-based ~53% prior.
