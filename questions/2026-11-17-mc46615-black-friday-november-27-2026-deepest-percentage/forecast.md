### Outside view

In a going-out-of-business (GOB) sale, liquidators start with shallow discounts and deepen them over an 8–12+ week term. The deepest levels (70–80%+) usually come in the final weeks: Party City reached up to 80% only in its last two weeks, and Big Lots was at 50–80% at its end stage [4]. This sale runs from Nov 6 2026 to Feb 28 2027 [5], so Black Friday falls in week 3 of about 16, which is early. In a normal year, Children's Place's own Black Friday promotion was "up to 70% off" [6]. The status quo is 60, and the ceiling of the base rate in the same period is about 70.

### Inside view

- **For staying at 60:** the homepage is already badged "Black Friday" at a 60% maximum, and the clearance tile is already at 60. Liquidators have little reason to cut deeply in week 3 when they still have 13 weeks to run.
- **For moving above 60:** the liquidators have said new markdowns are "expected as the sale progresses" [2]. Black Friday is the biggest traffic day of the year, and a clearance figure of "60–70%" or "up to 70%" is a natural next step. The site's promotions rotate quickly.
- **Smaller paths:**
  - Wording changes to "up to 75%".
  - The site drops the range wording and shows only "40% off", which would resolve below 60.
  - The capture is broken or the figures exist only in images, which pushes resolution to another capture.
