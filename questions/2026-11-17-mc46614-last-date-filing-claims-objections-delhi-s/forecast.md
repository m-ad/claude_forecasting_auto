## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-17

### What resolves YES

The option containing the last date for filing claims and objections (Form 6 for individual electors governs if dates differ) in Delhi's SIR, per the latest-dated official ECI / CEO Delhi document dated on or before Nov 27, 2026, 23:59 IST. Baseline: ECI notification 23/2026-ERS of Oct 29 → Nov 25. No revision by Nov 27 → "Nov 25 or earlier". An extension changing only Annexure-A form submission, house-to-house verification or disposal does **not** count. Arithmetic: today is Nov 17, 8 days before the Nov 25 deadline and 10 before the cut-off; past extensions were notified 1–4 days before the deadline in force (Sep 26 for Sep 30; Oct 29 for Oct 30), so a revision would most likely be dated Nov 21–25. Bins: +1 to +9 days → Nov 26–Dec 4; +10 to +20 days (e.g. "two weeks" → Dec 9) → Dec 5–15; ≥ +21 days (e.g. one month → Dec 25) → Dec 16+. Open-ended extension → Dec 16+.

### Outside view

Delhi's SIR has had every deadline moved: enumeration (at least twice), claims and objections twice (Sep 30 → Oct 30 → Nov 25, +30 and +26 days). For an Indian SIR with large exclusions under Supreme Court scrutiny, deadline extensions near expiry have been the norm in 2025–26. Base rate for "another extension at this stage", ~70%. Typical size ~4 weeks.

### Inside view

The decisive fact: on Nov 6 the ECI ordered a fresh door-to-door round of Annexure-A enumeration forms for the ~47.5 lakh electors left off the draft roll, and distribution was only to begin the week of Nov 16 (ThePrint/PTI Nov 12). ~300 voters per BLO cannot be reached, verified and turned into claims by Nov 25; the claims window almost certainly has to follow. The Supreme Court (hearing Nov 19) has already said ECI may be asked to extend. Raise P(extension of the filing date by Nov 27) to ~82%. Residual risk: ECI extends only the Annexure-A/verification phase or disposal date (excluded by the fine print), or acts after Nov 27.

Size: reporting (TOI Nov 12, Tribune Nov 13) of "around two weeks" under consideration → ~Dec 9, the Dec 5–15 bin. But prior extensions were ~1 month, final roll is Dec 22 and disposal Dec 15 would also have to move, and the fresh enumeration is a large job, so a one-month-ish extension (Dec 16+) is a strong alternative. A short (<10 day) extension is less likely but possible.

### Evidence

- Oct 29: ECI revised Delhi SIR — claims to Nov 25, disposal Dec 15, final roll Dec 22, a day after SC asked ECI to consider more time [1][2].
- Sep 26: one-month extension to Oct 30 at CEO Delhi's request [6].
- Nov 4: SC listed Form 6 dispute for Nov 19 [3].
- Nov 12: fresh enumeration forms for ~47.5 lakh excluded voters to be distributed from the week of Nov 16; no end date [4].
- Nov 12–13: TOI / Tribune report ~2-week extension under consideration, not approved (per question background) [5].
- No search found any further extension or a "no extension" statement as of Nov 17.
