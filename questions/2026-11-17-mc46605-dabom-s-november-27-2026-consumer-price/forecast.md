### Inside view

- **Bearish (−80 won on the median):** farm-gate is about 5,000 per 30 eggs (1,682 per 10 in early November, −2.3%; KREI expects another −20 to −40 per 10 by mid-November). The layer flock is a record and production is up 2–3% y/y. The retail gap is about 2,200–2,500 won, under political pressure (the layer association's request for a Fair Trade Commission probe), and retailers are expected to pass the farm-gate fall through gradually [1][3][5].
- **Bullish tail:** three layer-farm HPAI (highly pathogenic avian influenza) cases in 10 days, ~0.8M birds culled in Gyeonggi. Another large layer-farm outbreak could add a 300–500 won step, as the Nov 5 jump showed. I found no new layer case after Nov 12 [2][4].
- Retail prices fall stickily, so I put a modest median of 7,160 and a right-skewed upper tail.
