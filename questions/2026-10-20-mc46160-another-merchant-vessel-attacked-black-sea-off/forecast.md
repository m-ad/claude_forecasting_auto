## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-10-20

### What resolves YES

YES only if credible sources report that a merchant vessel (a civilian cargo ship or tanker) was hit by a drone, missile, mine or other munition **on 29 October 2026 (UTC)**. The vessel must be in or near the Black Sea territorial waters, EEZ (exclusive economic zone) or approach/anchorage waters of Bulgaria or Romania, and the hit must cause physical damage, a fire, a sinking or crew casualties. Strikes on warships, on port infrastructure, or on ships in Ukrainian or Russian waters or ports do not count. Neither do accidental fires, collisions or groundings, or strikes dated 28 or 30 October, even if they are reported on the 29th. Attribution is not required. The tracker blacksea.america2.news is a reference aid. In practice the question asks whether one specific UTC day, nine days from now, will be a strike day in the western Black Sea.

### Outside view

Two reference classes:
- **Before the wave (Jul–Sep 2026):** about one munition strike a month on merchant ships off Romania. Gas Lisbon was hit on 21 Jul and Progress IV sank on 27 Aug, both off Sfântu Gheorghe [5][6]. Before July 2026 I found almost none. That is about **3% per day**.
- **During the October wave (5–20 Oct):** 7 ships were hit on 5 distinct strike dates (5, 6, 12/13, 14 and 15 Oct) out of 16 days, about **30% per day**. The strikes come in clusters with gaps of 5–6 days [1][2][3][4]. No ship has been confirmed hit from 16 to 20 Oct.

Which class applies on 29 Oct depends on whether the campaign is still running. Waves like this usually decay or pause once the political cost rises.

### Inside view

- **For continuation:** Russia appears to be targeting Ukraine-bound and Danube-bound traffic that anchors off Sulina, and that motive has not changed. A NATO source told Reuters the drones were Russian [3]. On 17 Oct Romanian F-16s shot down two Geran drones near Sulina, so attempts went on after the last confirmed hit [7]. Russia rejected the Turkish and Ukrainian moratorium proposals in September [8], and no moratorium had been agreed as of 20 Oct. Ships still anchor off Sulina, where they are easy targets.
- **Against:** No ship has been confirmed hit for 5 days (16–20 Oct). Romania now shoots drones down instead of escorting them. A special North Atlantic Council session on 20 Oct is expected to add counter-drone assets [7][9]. Political pressure from Turkey, Bulgaria and the EU is rising, and autumn weather is getting worse. The question also needs a confirmed munition hit on one exact UTC day.
- **Rough model:** P(campaign still active around 29 Oct) ≈ 0.5–0.6. If it is active, the chance of a strike day is ≈ 0.20–0.25, lower than the wave average because of better air defence and the clustering. That gives ≈ 0.11–0.14. Adding the background rate of ≈ 0.03 gives **about 14%**.

### Evidence

- 5 Oct: Royad Mammadov sank off Romania; 2 dead [1][2].
- 6 Oct: Alfa Watan sank and Able was set ablaze in Bulgaria's EEZ [1][9].
- 12/13 Oct: Chelsea 3 was hit and HS Dignity sank off Bulgaria; 11 missing [1][4].
- 14 Oct: tanker Yara J caught fire about 10 nmi (nautical miles) SE of Sulina, inside Romanian waters. The defence minister said radar saw no drone; President Dan later said drones hit it [3][10].
- 15 Oct: tanker Wine Trader was hit 13 nmi SE of Sulina; 7 missing [3][10].
- 17 Oct: Romanian F-16s shot down two Russian-type Geran drones over Romanian waters near Sulina [1][7].
- 16–20 Oct: no confirmed new ship strike, per the tracker, the scouts and my searches. Weekend under-coverage is possible.
- Diplomacy: Bulgaria and Turkey are pushing a moratorium. Russia called it "unrealistic" in September [8]. Romania wants a joint NATO Black Sea command [7].

### What would change my mind

- **Upward:** a new strike off Sulina or Bulgaria between 20 and 28 Oct would show the campaign is still active, and I would put this at about 25–30%.
- **Downward:** an announced moratorium, NATO escorts or air cover over the Sulina anchorage, or traffic halted at Sulina would point to well below 10%.
