### Outside view

Reference class: Gallup Korea weekly readings for Lee over the last ~10 weeks, a period with no trend. Since mid-September the series has been 37, 37, 39, 40, 39, 41, 39. The mean of the last five readings is 39.6. With a ~±3.1 pt sampling margin (SD ≈ 1.6 pts) and small real drift, the week-to-week change runs 1–2 pts. A two-week change on a flat series has an SD of about 2 pts. Shocks of 4–5 pts in a week have happened (−4 in Nov 2025, +5 in Feb 2026), which fattens the tails. Base distribution: centred at ~39.3, SD ~2.0.

### Inside view

- **Flat regime.** Gallup itself describes the rating as essentially flat around 40% for a month. The last reading (39) sits slightly below the five-week mean (39.6), and a sampling dip tends to revert part-way.
- **Downside risks inside the window.** Han Duck-soo's likely release on 20 Nov (detention expires, no sentencing set), an escalating Democratic Party fight with Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae (impeachment is opposed by a majority, outside the margin of error), and stalled housing-supply bills while real estate remains the top disapproval reason. These add a little lower-tail mass. None looks like a large shock to the president's own rating.
- **Upside.** Diplomacy drove the 6 Nov rebound to 41. I found no scheduled diplomatic event inside the window (speculative: a year-end summit could add 1–2 pts).
- **Cross-check against other pollsters.** Realmeter has Lee at 35.9% (2–6 Nov, down 1.0). It runs 3–5 pts below Gallup and shows the same flat-to-slightly-soft picture.

Result: median 39.2, a slightly fat lower tail, IQR 38.0–40.4 (in practice, mass concentrated on 38/39/40).
