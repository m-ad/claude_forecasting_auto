### Outside view
Reference class: Israeli withdrawals from territory held in an active conflict zone before a counterpart's demanded precondition is met, within ~3 months, absent a signed agreement being implemented. Related cases: Gaza 2005 (took years of preparation), Lebanon 2000 (announced months earlier), Israeli pullbacks after the 2025 Gaza ceasefire (executed within 24h of cabinet approval, Oct 2025), Lebanon post-Nov-2024 ceasefire (60-day deadline missed; troops kept 5 hilltop positions into 2026 — from memory, not verified this session). Since the Oct 2025 ceasefire, the line has moved *outward* only (53% → 60% → 65–70%). Base rate for a retreat of a line that the army calls a "new border" (Zamir, Dec 2025) within 80 days: low single digits. Status-quo prior ≈ 3%.

### Inside view
YES needs a chain: (1) Hamas begins verifiable decommissioning or Israel waives sequencing under US pressure (~15% by mid-Dec: roadmap agreed 31 Jul, stalled; ISF unassembled; NCAG not in Gaza); (2) Israel accepts a first-stage pullback to the *original* line (~30% given 1, since Board of Peace plan reportedly includes it and Trump pressure is real; Netanyahu publicly rejects); (3) it is executed and credibly verified before Jan 1 (~60%). Product ≈ 2.7%. Alternative path: the Israeli election (27 Oct) produces a government (Eisenkot-led) willing to pull back — coalition formation takes weeks and the IDF would not move before a government exists, ≈ +0.5%. Resolution-ambiguity path (resolver counts Makovsky-style reports or a partial retreat) ≈ +1–2%. Total ≈ 4–5%.
