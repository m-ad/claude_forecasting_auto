## v1 — Initial forecast — 2026-11-16

### What resolves YES

The question resolves to the national cumulative number of postal **ballots** (not applications) that Correos reports as admitted, deposited or sent for the 29 November 2026 general election. The figure comes from the latest Correos press release dated 26 November 2026 or earlier (Madrid time), used exactly as stated. An approximate figure such as "más de 1,1 millones" counts as 1,100,000. If Correos publishes no such figure, the question is annulled. The calendar is: applications close 19 Nov and the deposit deadline is 25 Nov, so a release on 25 Nov (evening) or 26 Nov should be near-final. A release dated before the deadline would undercount by a few percent. Bounds are 500k–3M and both are open. Arithmetic: if applications are A, ballots ≈ 0.91–0.94 × A (6–9 % of applicants never vote by post, 2008–2023). For the ~1.0M applications CCOO (a trade union) expects, that gives about 0.92–0.94M. As of 16 Nov 2026, Correos has published no 29N count [1].

### Outside view

There are five reference elections for postal ballots admitted, all national except the European one. They are 10N 2019 (November, a repeat election with voter fatigue): about 0.92M [3]. April 2019: 1,241,716 (1.347M applications) [6]. June 2016: 1,357,745 [6]. July 2023 (summer holidays): 2,471,935 [2]. European election, June 2024: more than 571k by the evening before the deadline [7]. Postal voting rises with summer dates and with high salience. The 2023 surge has not carried over to non-summer contests: Andalucía's May 2026 applications were +1.1 % on 2022 [1]. Leaving out the summer and European cases, the November and spring range is 0.9–1.36M.

### Inside view

- **Up from November 2019:** this is a high-stakes snap election with a likely change of government (Feijóo about 84 % on Polymarket [8]). It is not a fourth-election rerun, and the PP's head office is campaigning to explain how to vote by post [5].
- **Down:** Abascal (Vox) and Ayuso urge voters not to vote by post [4]. Their voters lean towards in-person voting anyway. CCOO expects only about 1M applications [9].
- **Uncertainty:** Interior has released no 2026 figures [10], so there is no direct anchor. The resolving release may be interim (dated 25 Nov, before all ballots are counted) or rounded down ("más de 1 millón"). Both skew the outcome low.

Median 1.10M; IQR 0.95–1.28M; 5–95 % 0.74–1.68M. The upper tail allows for a partial 2023-style habit effect.

### Evidence

- 16 Nov 2026: Correos' latest release (application deadline reminder) has no figures [1].
- 10 Nov 2026: CCOO expects about 1 million postal-vote applications [9].
- 16 Nov 2026: Interior has published no postal-vote data, and the PP has asked the JEC (Junta Electoral Central, the central electoral board) for Correos' daily reports [10].
- May 2026 Andalucía election: 178,540 applications and 163,510 ballots admitted (91.6 %) [1].
