description: Red-team an existing forecast without changing it
argument-hint: <slug>
---

Red-team the forecast in `questions/$ARGUMENTS/` — **do not modify any file**.

Argue the strongest possible case for a materially different number. Specifically:

1. **Resolution risk** — re-read the criteria and fine print cold. Is there a reading under
   which this resolves the other way? Revisions, ties, "if no such event", cutoff dates?
2. **Reference class** — is the chosen class the right one? Name a rival class and its base rate.
3. **Conjunction** — does the thesis require several things to go right? Multiply them out.
4. **Anchoring** — how much of the number is inherited from the community prediction rather
   than derived? Would the forecast look different if the CP had been 20 pp away?
5. **Missing tripwires** — what could plausibly happen that nobody listed?
6. **IQR width** — is the band defensible? What does the historical spread of this quantity
   look like? Under-wide intervals are the most common calibration failure.

Finish with: the alternative forecast the red-team case implies, how much weight it deserves,
and the one piece of evidence that would settle it.
